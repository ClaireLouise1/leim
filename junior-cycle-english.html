How the exam works

One written paper in June of 3rd year, at Higher or Ordinary level, set and marked by the State Examinations Commission.

2 hoursone paper at each level
180marks for the paper
1 themeevery question links to the paper's theme, and the sections and their marks change from year to year
2 CBAsOral Communication (2nd year) and the Collection of the Student's Texts (3rd year); students sitting in 2027 and 2028 do only one

Because the layout changes every year, you can't learn the paper off by heart. What you can learn are the skills it always tests: reading closely, answering the exact question, backing up a point with a short quote, and writing clearly for a reader. That's what every Léim English topic practises.

What you study

Over 2nd and 3rd year your teacher picks the texts from the Department of Education's lists.

From the prescribed lists

2 novels, 2 drama texts (an extract or extracts can count as one) and 1 film.

Poems, stories and more

At least 16 poems, a number of short stories, and a range of non-literary texts such as articles, ads, speeches and web pages, including spoken ones.

Higher Level only

One full Shakespeare play.

Which list is yours?

Sitting the exam in 2027: Circular 0051/2024 (the same list as 2024–2026). Sitting in 2028, 2029 or 2030: the new list in Circular 0038/2025. Your teacher will know which texts your class is doing.

Does Léim cover my novel? Our English topics teach the skills the exam asks for (character, setting, theme, key scenes, point–quote–explain) using short original texts written by us. You then use the same skills on whichever novel, play or film your class studied.

The two CBAs

Classroom-Based Assessments are done in class, judged by your teacher and reported on the Junior Cycle Profile of Achievement (JCPA), separately from the exam.

CBA 1 · 2nd year

Oral Communication

You pick a topic and a format (on your own or in a group), research and prepare it, then present it to the class. You also write a short Student Reflection Note. Your teacher judges it against four descriptors.

CBA 2 · 3rd year

Collection of the Student's Texts

Over 2nd and 3rd year you build a collection of your own writing in different genres, choose pieces and redraft them, and write a Student Reflection Note. Judged against four descriptors.

Students sitting Junior Cycle in 2027 and 2028 complete only one CBA per subject, so ask the teacher which one your class is doing. The written Assessment Task linked to CBA 2 has not been required in recent years. Free guides for both CBAs are on the way.

Questions

Which specification do you follow?

The Junior Cycle English specification (2015, with small changes in 2018), which all current 1st, 2nd and 3rd years follow. Every game, sheet and pack is tagged with its learning outcome codes (for example W11), so you can match it to your child's school work.

Higher or Ordinary level?

The games and sheets work for both levels. Shakespeare is Higher Level only, and where a topic goes further at Higher Level we say so on the topic page.

Why aren't there games about my novel or play?

Schools choose different texts from the lists, and the exam asks about skills, not one set book. So each topic uses short texts written by us to practise a skill (for example how a writer shows character), and students then use that skill on their own texts.

Do students need an account?

No. Students just play. Parents and teachers can sign up for the free parent guide and news of new topics.

Are you connected to the SEC or the NCCA?

No. Léim is an independent revision service. All questions and texts are written by us in the style of the exam; we never copy past papers.
