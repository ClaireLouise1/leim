Describing people: adjectives that agree

Spot the pattern

  • Mi hermano es alto. (My brother is tall.)
  • Mi hermana es alta. (My sister is tall.)
  • Mis hermanos son altos. (My brothers are tall.)
  • Mis hermanas son altas. (My sisters are tall.)

What changes at the end of alto, and why? Think about it, then check.

Show me

In Spanish the adjective agrees with the person: -o for a boy or man, -a for a girl or woman, and add -s for more than one. The verb changes too: es for one person, son for more than one. A mixed group (boys and girls) uses the masculine: Mis abuelos son simpáticos.

Watch out: -e and -ista adjectives don't change for boys and girls, only for more than one. But -or adjectives do: mi hermana es trabajadora, mi amiga es habladora.

Looks: ser or tener?

Use ser (es / son)Use tener (tiene / tienen)
Mi padre es alto y guapo.Mi padre tiene el pelo corto y negro.
Mi prima es pelirroja.Mi prima tiene los ojos verdes.
Mis abuelos son bajos.Mi abuelo tiene barba.

Hair and eyes agree with el pelo and los ojos, not with the person. El pelo is masculine and singular, so it's always pelo largo, even for a girl: Mi madre tiene el pelo largo y rubio. Los ojos is plural, so: los ojos azules, verdes, marrones.

Hair: largo (long), corto (short), rizado (curly), liso (straight), rubio (blond), castaño (brown), negro (black). Eyes: azules, verdes, marrones, grises.

Personality words to learn

simpático (nice), antipático (unfriendly), divertido (fun), gracioso (funny), aburrido (boring), tímido (shy), serio (serious), generoso (generous), perezoso (lazy), cariñoso (affectionate), inteligente, amable (kind), paciente, alegre (cheerful), trabajador (hard-working), hablador (chatty), deportista (sporty), egoísta (selfish).

Make it sound natural with muy (very), bastante (quite), un poco (a bit, for negative things: un poco perezoso) and pero: Es simpático pero un poco serio.

Exam tip: in the Writing section, describing your best friend or a family member is a classic. Give looks + personality + a reason: Mi mejor amiga se llama Aoife. Es alta y tiene el pelo largo y rizado. Es muy divertida y generosa, pero a veces es un poco habladora. Before you finish, check every adjective: who is it describing, boy or girl, one or more? In Listening, opposites are the trap: alto/bajo, largo/corto, simpático/antipático.
