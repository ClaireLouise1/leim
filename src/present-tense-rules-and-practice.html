The present tense

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 2.1, 1.13, 1.20 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 2.1, 1.9, 1.15 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026). Play the game: leim.ie/verb-sprint.html

Use it for what you do usually or what is happening now: Vivo en Cork (I live in Cork). Juego al fútbol los sábados (I play football on Saturdays).

1. Regular verbs in 3 steps

① Take the infinitive (hablar, comer, vivir) ② take off -ar / -er / -ir ③ add the ending.

hablar (speak)comer (eat)vivir (live)
yohablocomovivo
túhablascomesvives
él / ella / ustedhablacomevive
nosotros/ashablamoscomemosvivimos
vosotros/ashabláiscoméisvivís
ellos / ellas / ustedeshablancomenviven
Spot the pattern: -er and -ir share endings except nosotros (-emos / -imos) and vosotros (-éis / -ís). Yo always ends in -o.

2. Irregular verbs: learn them in groups

The big three (learn by heart)

serestarir
yosoyestoyvoy
túeresestásvas

"Go" verbs: only yo is odd.
hacer → hago · salir → salgo · poner → pongo · tener → tengo · venir → vengo · decir → digo

"Boot" verbs: the vowel changes in every form except nosotros and vosotros.
e → ie: quiero, tienes, vienes
o → ue: puedo, duermo
u → ue: juego
e → i: dices
…but queremos, podemos, jugamos, decimos.

ver: veo, ves, ve, vemos, veis, ven

Exam tip: in the Writing section, check every verb before you hand up: who is doing it, and does the ending match? Yo → -o is the quickest mark to win (or lose).
