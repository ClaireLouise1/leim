Simultaneous equations

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): AF.4, U.6. Play the game: leim.ie/two-line-crossing.html

1. What is the solution?

Each equation on its own has lots of answers. The solution is the one pair (x, y) that fits both. On a graph it's where the lines cross: x + y = 5 (l) and y = 2x − 1 (k) cross at (2, 3).

2. Elimination

Make the x's or y's the same size, then signs different → add, signs the same → subtract.

① 2x + 3y = 12   ② 5x − 2y = 11
① × 2: 4x + 6y = 24   ② × 3: 15x − 6y = 33
Add: 19x = 57, x = 3. In ①: 6 + 3y = 12, y = 2
Check ②: 15 − 4 = 11 ✓

Multiply every term, including the number on the right.

3. Substitution

When one equation says y = …, put it into the other. Keep the bracket.

① y = 2x − 3   ② 3x + 2y = 15
3x + 2(2x − 3) = 15 → 7x − 6 = 15 → x = 3
y = 2(3) − 3 = 3. Check ②: 9 + 6 = 15 ✓

4. Word problems

Say what x and y stand for, make one equation from each fact, solve, then answer in words. 120 tickets, adults €8, children €5, €801: x + y = 120 and 8x + 5y = 801 → 67 adults, 53 children.

5. Higher Level

Fractions in an equation: multiply every term by the common denominator (x/2 + y/3 = 4 → 3x + 2y = 24). Answers can be fractions (x = 3/2).

Classic mistakes: adding when the signs are the same · not multiplying the number on the right · dropping the bracket in substitution · finding x but not y · a pair that fits only one equation.
Exam tip: label the equations ① and ②, write each step ("① × 2", "① + ②"), find both letters, and write the check in the other equation.

  2. Is x = 2, y = 5 a solution of 3x + y = 11 and x − y = −2?
  3. 2x + 3y = 19 and x = 5. Find y.
  4. x − 4y = 2 and y = −1. Find x.
  5. Which of (1, 6), (2, 4) and (3, 2) fits both 2x + y = 8 and x + y = 5?
  6. Two numbers add up to 10 and their difference is 4. Find them.

E. Exam-style questions (show your work)

  1. Solve and check. Solve 3x − 2y = 4 and 5x + 3y = 13. Check your answer in both equations.
  2. Graph and algebra. On a grid, draw the lines y = x + 1 and y = −x + 5 for x from 0 to 5. (a) Write down where they cross. (b) Solve the equations with algebra to check.
  3. Match tickets. 200 people went to a match. Adult tickets cost €12 and child tickets cost €7. The takings were €1,890. (a) Write two equations. (b) How many children went?
  4. Substitution. (a) Solve y = 2x − 1 and 4x − 3y = 1. (b) Higher Level: solve 3x/2 − y = 2 and x + y/2 = 6.
