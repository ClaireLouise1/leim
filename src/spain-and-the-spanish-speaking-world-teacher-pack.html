Junior Cycle Spanish · Teacher pack

Spain and the Spanish-speaking world

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 3.1–3.9 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 3.1–3.9 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can name Spanish-speaking countries, capitals and nationalities (with agreement: mexicano / mexicana / mexicanos), describe a festival (se celebra en…, es el…, la gente…, es típico…), compare Spain or Latin America with Ireland (más… que, menos… que, tan… como) and tell a fact from a stereotype.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Fiesta Fact Files (A/B info gap) · p.3–4 Culture Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster with maps · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 2nd–3rd year. Time: about 60 minutes. All texts are original.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: ¿Verdad o estereotipo? Read out four statements (Todos los españoles bailan flamenco. · En España se cena muy tarde. · En Irlanda llueve todos los días. · Más de 600 millones de personas hablan español.). Students stand up for a stereotype, stay seated for a fact. Ask: what makes something a stereotype?
8 minGuided discovery. Board: Frida es mexicana. · Diego es mexicano. · Aoife es irlandesa. · Mis primos (Ana y Luis) son colombianos. Pairs work out the rules: capital letter? -o/-a? what happens to irlandés? boys + girls? Then show the poster (p.5): find five countries and their capitals on the maps.
15 minFiesta Fact Files (p.2). Pairs, A and B cards. Students ask about the three festivals they don't know and write the answers; then agree which festival they'd most like to go to and why. Monitor the questions (¿Dónde se celebra…?). Extension: pairs present "their" fiesta to another pair, comparing it with an Irish festival.
10 minCulture Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Answer chain below.
12 minWorksheet (p.6): read Diego's email, A alone, B–C in pairs. Feedback: why does Niamh need warm clothes in June? (it's winter in Peru)
5 minExit ticket. One sentence comparing Spain or Latin America with Ireland (En España… más… que en Irlanda) and one nationality with a capital (Es peruana. La capital de Perú es Lima.). Homework: play Fiesta Quiz at leim.ie/fiesta-quiz.html and copy five lines from the "Go back over these" list.
Fact File answers: A learns from B: San Fermín, Pamplona, 6–14 July, white clothes and red scarf, run in front of the bulls, the encierro is at 8 am · Inti Raymi, Cusco, 24 June, actors in colourful costumes act out an Inca ceremony, festival of the Sun, the Inca speaks Quechua · La Tomatina, Buñol near Valencia, last Wednesday of August, people throw tomatoes for an hour, started in 1945. B learns from A: Las Fallas, Valencia, 15–19 March, huge figures burnt on the last night, one figure is saved · Día de Muertos, Mexico, 1–2 November, altars with flowers, candles and photos, cemetery, pan de muerto · Nochevieja, Puerta del Sol, Madrid, 31 December, twelve grapes at midnight, chimes on TV.
Errors to listen for: Es Mexicana (→ no capital) · irlandésa (→ irlandesa) · más grande como (→ que) · tan grande que (→ como) · la gente bailan (→ baila) · en el julio (→ en julio). Language awareness: Latin America uses ustedes for all "you plural"; Argentina uses vos. Support: leave the poster up. Fast finishers: write a Fact File row for St Patrick's Day.

Worksheet

Diego's Inti Raymi

↗ Léim

Diego lives in Cusco, Peru. Niamh, an Irish student, is going to stay with his family in June.

¡Hola, Niamh! ¡Qué bien que vienes a Cusco en junio! Vas a llegar justo para el Inti Raymi, la fiesta más importante de mi ciudad. Es el 24 de junio y es la antigua fiesta inca del Sol. Por la mañana hay una ceremonia en el templo de Qorikancha y después todos vamos a Sacsayhuamán, una fortaleza inca en las montañas. Allí cientos de actores, con trajes de muchos colores, representan la ceremonia en quechua, la lengua de los incas. Mi abuela habla quechua y español, ¡y yo estoy aprendiendo!
Trae ropa de abrigo: en junio es invierno aquí y por la noche hace mucho frío, a veces cero grados. Cusco está a más de tres mil metros de altura, así que los primeros días tienes que caminar despacio y beber mucha agua. Después de la fiesta podemos ir a Machu Picchu en tren. Mi madre dice que vamos a comer cuy, un plato típico de los Andes… ¡pero no te preocupes, también hay pizza!
¿Cómo es tu ciudad? ¿Hace más frío en Irlanda que en Cusco? ¿Hay fiestas como el Inti Raymi? Un abrazo, Diego

A. Answer in English

  1. Why is June a good time for Niamh to arrive?
  2. What is the Inti Raymi?
  3. Where do people go after the morning ceremony?
  4. What language is the ceremony in? Who speaks it in Diego's family?
  1. Why should Niamh bring warm clothes?
  2. What should she do for the first few days, and why?
  3. How will they get to Machu Picchu?
  4. What three questions does Diego ask?

B. Find the Spanish in the email

  1. the most important festival in my city
  2. bring warm clothes
  1. more than three thousand metres high
  2. don't worry

C. Compare Cusco and Cork (más / menos / tan… que / como)

  1. Cusco está ______ alto ______ Cork.
  2. En junio, en Cusco hace ______ frío ______ en Cork.
  1. El Inti Raymi es ______ famoso ______ San Patricio. (= as… as)
  2. Para mí, la pizza es ______ que el cuy. (= better)

D. Now you

Reply to Diego as Niamh (50–60 words): thank him, answer his three questions, describe one Irish festival (when, where, what people do) and say what you'd like to do in Peru.

Answers. A: 1 She'll arrive just in time for the Inti Raymi, the most important festival in Cusco. 2 The old Inca festival of the Sun, on 24 June. 3 To Sacsayhuamán, an Inca fortress in the mountains. 4 Quechua, the language of the Incas; his grandmother (and Diego is learning it). 5 It's winter in June and it's very cold at night, sometimes zero degrees. 6 Walk slowly and drink lots of water, because Cusco is more than 3,000 metres high. 7 By train. 8 What is your town like? Is it colder in Ireland than in Cusco? Are there festivals like the Inti Raymi? · B: 9 la fiesta más importante de mi ciudad 10 trae ropa de abrigo 11 a más de tres mil metros de altura 12 no te preocupes · C: 13 más… que 14 más… que (in June it's winter in Cusco) 15 tan… como 16 mejor
Watch out: la fortaleza = fortress; de abrigo = warm (clothes); la altura = altitude; el cuy = guinea pig, a traditional Andean dish. Seasons are the other way round south of the equator.
