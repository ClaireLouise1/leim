Junior Cycle Spanish · Teacher pack

Shopping, clothes & prices

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 1.11, 1.14, 3.10 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 1.4, 1.10, 2.6 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can name everyday clothes, make colours agree (una falda roja, unos guantes azules), ask and answer ¿Cuánto cuesta / cuestan?, say and understand prices in euro (including the listening traps quince / cincuenta), use este / ese, compare prices (más barato que) and manage a short conversation in a clothes shop.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Moda Luna info gap + role-play (two cards) · p.3–4 Shopping Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 1st–3rd year. Time: about 60 minutes.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: The Price Is Right. Hold up (or show pictures of) four items of clothing. Students guess the price in Spanish; you say the real price (Cuesta veinticinco euros con noventa y nueve) and they write it in figures on mini-whiteboards. Slip in quince / cincuenta and sesenta / setenta.
10 minGuided discovery. Board: un jersey negro · una falda negra · unos zapatos negros · unas botas negras and un bolso azul · unos guantes azules. "Where does the colour go? Why does negro change four times but azul only twice?" Then ¿Cuánto cuesta la gorra? / ¿Cuánto cuestan las botas?: "Why the -n?" (one thing / more than one, like gusta / gustan). Use classroom objects for este bolígrafo / esa mochila. Concept checks: "una camiseta rojo: right or wrong?" "Boots: cuesta or cuestan?"
5 minPoster (p.5). Drill the colours and the four forms of este; chant the number pairs (quince-cincuenta, catorce-cuarenta…) getting faster.
15 minModa Luna info gap + role-play (p.2), pairs A and B. Step 1: students ask about colours and prices to complete the catalogue. Step 2: two short shop role-plays with a budget, then swap roles. Check: the hoodie is 29,50 €, the skirt comes in red or green, the coat is 89 €.
10 minShopping Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Players match the Spanish to the English. Watch cuesta / cuestan and the prices. Answer chain below.
10 minWorksheet (p.6) A and B in pairs. Feed back on the error correction: students say why (agreement? plural? word order?).
5 minExit ticket. Describe what you're wearing today (three items, three colours) and say how much one thing cost. Homework: play Price Check at leim.ie/price-check.html and copy five sentences from the "Go back over these" list.
Errors to listen for: una camiseta rojo (roja) · un rojo jersey (un jersey rojo) · ¿Cuánto cuesta los zapatos? (cuestan) · esto jersey (este) · azuls / marróns (azules, marrones) · Los pantalones me queda grande (me quedan grandes) · más barato como (que) · veintiuno euros (veintiún) · writing 50 for quince in listening.
Language awareness: Irish also puts the colour after the noun and changes it: léine dhearg (a red shirt), bróga dearga (red shoes). Point this out: students already do agreement every day. Support: give weaker students the poster as a mat. Fast finishers: design a sale advert for Moda Luna with "antes" and "ahora" prices for a partner to read aloud.

¿Cuánto cuesta?

↗ Léim

Card A · Moda Luna

Step 1: you and your partner have the same catalogue from Moda Luna, but you only know half of it (cream boxes). Ask and answer in full sentences to fill your gaps: ¿Cuánto cuesta la falda? – Cuesta veinticuatro euros. ¿De qué colores tienen los vaqueros? – Los tenemos azules o negros. Write prices in figures. Worksheet

Álvaro's email from A Coruña

↗ Léim

A. Read Álvaro's email

¡Hola, Aoife! ¿Qué tal? Ayer fui de compras con mi madre y mi hermana Inés a un centro comercial de A Coruña. En enero hay rebajas y todo está más barato. Yo necesitaba ropa para el instituto: en mi instituto no llevamos uniforme, pero mi madre dice que mis vaqueros están muy viejos. Compré unos vaqueros negros por veinticinco euros (antes costaban cuarenta) y una sudadera gris con capucha. La sudadera me quedaba un poco grande, pero la dependienta me dio una talla más pequeña. Inés buscaba unas botas marrones, pero eran demasiado caras: ¡noventa euros! Al final compró unas zapatillas blancas, que eran más baratas. Mi madre no compró nada porque "solo estaba mirando". Normalmente prefiero la ropa deportiva porque es cómoda, y no me gusta la ropa de marca porque es muy cara. ¿Sabías que Zara es de A Coruña? La primera tienda abrió aquí en 1975. ¿Y tú? ¿Llevas uniforme? ¿Qué ropa te gusta? Un abrazo, Álvaro
  1. Who did Álvaro go shopping with?
  2. Why is everything cheaper?
  3. Why did he need new clothes for school?
  4. What did he buy?
  5. How much were the jeans, and how much were they before?
  6. What was the problem with the hoodie, and what happened?
  7. What was Inés looking for, and why didn't she buy it?
  8. What did she buy in the end?
  9. What clothes does Álvaro prefer, and why?
  10. What does he say about Zara?

B. Find and fix the mistake

  1. La camiseta cuestan diez euros.
  2. Llevo una falda negro.
  3. Estos chaqueta es muy bonita.
  4. Me gustan los zapatos marróns.
  1. Los vaqueros me queda pequeños.
  2. La gorra es más barata como la bufanda.
  3. Quiero un azul jersey.
  4. Cuesta veintiuno euros.

C. Now you

Write Aoife's reply to Álvaro (50–70 words). Answer his two questions (describe your uniform with colours), and say what you bought the last time you went shopping and how much it cost.

Answers. A: 1 His mum and his sister Inés. 2 It's January: the sales are on. 3 His mum says his jeans are very old (there's no uniform). 4 Black jeans and a grey hoodie. 5 €25; €40 before. 6 It was a bit big; the shop assistant gave him a smaller size. 7 Brown boots; they were too expensive (€90). 8 White trainers (cheaper). 9 Sportswear, because it's comfortable. 10 It's from A Coruña; the first shop opened there in 1975. · B: 11 cuesta 12 negra 13 Esta 14 marrones 15 quedan 16 que 17 un jersey azul 18 veintiún.
Watch out: la sudadera con capucha = hoodie; me quedaba grande = it was too big for me; la dependienta = the shop assistant; al final = in the end; ¿Sabías que…? = Did you know that…?
