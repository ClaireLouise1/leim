1. Index notation

In 2⁵, 2 is the base and 5 is the index: 2⁵ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32 (not 10).

10⁶ = 1,000,000 (6 zeros). (−3)² = 9 but −3² = −9: the index only belongs to what it touches.

2. Square roots

√49 = 7 because 7² = 49. 49½ = √49. √50 is between 7 and 8 (7² = 49, 8² = 64). √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10, not 6 + 8.

3. BIMDAS

Brackets, Indices, ÷ ×, + −
5 + 3² × 2 = 5 + 9 × 2 = 5 + 18 = 23
4 × 2³ = 4 × 8 = 32  (not 8³)

4. Standard form

a × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ a < 10 and n a whole number.

45,000 → 4.5 (the point moved 4 places) → 4.5 × 10⁴
3.07 × 10⁵ → move the point 5 right → 307,000

Not standard form: 45 × 10³, 0.45 × 10⁵. Compare the powers of 10 first.

5. The laws of indices

aᵖ × aq = ap+qadd
aᵖ ÷ aq = ap−qsubtract
(aᵖ)q = apqmultiply

x³ × x⁵ = x⁸ · 5⁹ ÷ 5³ = 5⁶ · (2³)⁴ = 2¹². The base stays the same: 2³ × 2⁴ = 2⁷, not 4⁷.

Numbers in front: 3x² × 4x⁵ = 12x⁷ · 12x⁷ ÷ 3x² = 4x⁵. x³ + x⁴ can't be joined.

Find n: 2ⁿ × 2³ = 2¹⁰ → n + 3 = 10 → n = 7.

6. Calculating in standard form

(4 × 10³) × (5 × 10²) = 20 × 10⁵ = 2 × 10⁶
(8 × 10⁹) ÷ (2 × 10³) = 4 × 10⁶
numbers first, then the indices, then tidy up

7. Zero, negative & fractional Higher Level

a⁰ = 1 · 2⁻³ = 1/2³ = 1/8 (not negative) · 81/3 = 2 · 272/3 = 3² = 9 (root first) · (2x³)² = 4x⁶ · 2ˣ = 1/8 = 2⁻³, so x = −3 · 0.00036 = 3.6 × 10⁻⁴

Exam tip: write the law you used (2⁵ × 2³ = 2⁵⁺³ = 2⁸), give the answer in the form asked for ("as a power of 2", "in standard form"), and copy calculator answers in standard form properly with × 10 and the index.
