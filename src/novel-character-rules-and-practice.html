Learning outcomes (2015 specification): R6, R7, R4, W8. Play the game: leim.ie/character-clues.html

1. Show, don't tell

Writers rarely say "Mia is kind". They show it, and you find the clues: “Mia gave half of her lunch to the new boy.”

2. STEAL: five ways to show a character

Speechwhat they say, and how: “It should have been me.”
Thoughtswhat goes on in their head: He wished he had trained harder.
Effect on othershow other people react: The others groaned but did what she said.
Actionswhat they do: He handed the wallet in.
Lookswhat they look like or wear: His jumper was creased.

3. Words for what a character is like

kind · brave · shy · bossy · selfish · lazy · honest · jealous · funny · clever · rude
Stronger words for the exam: generous, loyal, determined, confident, arrogant, sly, caring, hard-working, stubborn.

4. Trait or feeling?

A trait is what someone is like (it lasts): brave, honest.
A feeling is how they feel right now (it passes): scared, happy.
Character questions want traits.

5. Point, quote, explain

Point: Ciarán is selfish.
Quote: he “grabbed the biggest slice”
Explain: he takes the most before anyone else sits down, so he thinks only of himself.

6. Characters change

Say what they are like at the start and at the end, with a quote for each. Leah: shy (“stared at her shoes”) → brave (“walked on stage”).

Classic mistakes: a feeling instead of a trait · a point with no quote · a quote that doesn't prove the point · copying a whole sentence · two traits with only one quote · describing what happens instead of what the character is like.
Exam tip: one trait per paragraph, each with its own short quote. "Two qualities" means two separate points.

Worked examples

Fionn, line 2: Thoughts“For a second he thought about the new football boots he wanted.” We see inside his head: he is tempted. That makes handing the wallet in (line 3) even more honest.
Leah, line 3: Effect on others“Nobody in the new class knew she could sing.” The line is about other people, not Leah's own words or actions. Their surprise at the talent show is coming.
Trait or feeling?“Scared” is how Niamh might feel in the river. “Brave” is what she is like: she is scared and still “waded in after it”.
Aisling: two traits, two quotesBossy: “and nobody talks”. Clever: she “worked out which way was north”. One quote can't prove both.
