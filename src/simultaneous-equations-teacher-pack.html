Simultaneous equations

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): AF.4, U.6

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can check whether a pair of values solves two equations, solve simultaneous linear equations by elimination, by substitution and from a graph, check the answer in the other equation, and form and solve simultaneous equations from a word problem. Higher Level: fractions in the equations and answers that are fractions.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Crossing Point mingle cards + Ticket Swap A/B cards · p.3 Simultaneous Dominoes (cut-up) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Error Hunt + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 3rd year (Higher and Ordinary). Time: about 60 minutes. All questions are original.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: think of two numbers. "I'm thinking of two numbers. They add up to 10." Pairs list all the whole-number answers. "Their difference is 4." Which pair now? Elicit that two facts pin down one pair.
8 minGuided discovery. Pairs make a table of values for x + y = 5 and for y = 2x − 1 (x = 0 to 4), circle the pair in both tables, then plot both lines on one grid. "Where is your circled pair on the graph?" Then: "Add x + y = 5 to x − y = 1. What happens to y?" Build elimination from there.
10 minCrossing Point mingle (p.2, 24 cards = 12 question/answer pairs; one card each). Question card? Solve it, then find the person with your answer, written as (x, y). Answer card? Find your pair of equations. Pairs check the answer in both equations.
12 minTicket Swap (p.2, pairs, don't show your card). Each fact is on only ONE card. Ask your partner for what you're missing ("How much is a child ticket?"), write the equations together, then both solve. Feedback: 150 tickets → 80 adult and 70 child; hot chocolate €3, muffin €4.
10 minSimultaneous Dominoes (p.3), groups of 3–4. Start with START, solve the clue, find the domino with that answer on its left, and build the chain to FINISH. Answers are written as (x, y) unless the clue asks for one letter.
12 minWorksheet (p.5). Error Hunt in pairs (find the mistake, name it, fix it), then the exam-style questions alone. Question 5 is Higher Level.
3 minExit ticket. Solve x + y = 9 and x − y = 3 and check your answer. (x = 6, y = 3.) Homework: play Two-Line Crossing at leim.ie/two-line-crossing.html and copy three lines from the "Go back over these" list.
Mingle key (question card → answer card): 13 → 12 · 11 → 2 · 23 → 14 · 9 → 7 · 3 → 17 · 8 → 24 · 15 → 4 · 10 → 21 · 6 → 20 · 19 → 16 · 1 → 18 · 22 → 5.
Errors to look for: adding when the signs are the same · not multiplying the number on the right · dropping the bracket in substitution · finding x but not y · not checking in the other equation · totals swapped in word problems. Support: a card with "signs different → add, signs the same → subtract". Fast finishers: make up two equations whose solution is x = −2, y = 5 and swap with a partner.

  1. Solve 4x + 3y = 18 and 2x − 5y = −4. Check your answer.
  2. The lines l: y = x + 1 and k: x + y = 5 are shown. (a) Use the graph to solve the equations. (b) Check your answer with algebra.

  4. Solve y = 10 − 3x and 2x − y = 5.
  5. Higher Level: (a) solve x/3 + y/4 = 4 and x − y = −2. (b) Solve 6x + 4y = 7 and 2x − 4y = 1.

B. Exam-style questions

  1. ② × 2: 4x − 10y = −8; subtract from ①: 13y = 26, y = 2, x = 3. Check: 6 − 10 = −4 ✓
  2. (a) x = 2, y = 3 (b) x + (x + 1) = 5 → 2x = 4 → x = 2, y = 3 ✓
  3. (a) 3x + 2y = 14, 2x + 3y = 16 (b) × 3 and × 2: 9x + 6y = 42, 4x + 6y = 32; subtract: 5x = 10: sandwich €2, juice €4
  4. 2x − (10 − 3x) = 5 → 5x = 15 → x = 3, y = 1
  5. (a) × 12: 4x + 3y = 48; x = y − 2 → 7y = 56 → y = 8, x = 6 (b) add: 8x = 8, x = 1; 2 − 4y = 1, y = 1/4

Marking tip

Give credit for each step, as the exam does: a correct method to get rid of one letter, the first value, the second value, and the check. An answer with x only is not finished. In word problems insist on "x = adult tickets" at the start and a sentence at the end.
