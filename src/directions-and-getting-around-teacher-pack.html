Junior Cycle Spanish · Teacher pack

Directions and getting around

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 1.3, 1.5, 1.11 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 1.4, 1.10, 1.16 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can ask the way politely (Perdone, ¿para ir al…?), understand and give directions with tú and usted (sigue / siga, gira / gire, toma / tome, cruza / cruce), use primera, segunda, tercera, al / a la / del and place phrases (enfrente de, al lado de, en la esquina), say how they travel (en autobús, a pie) and cope at a station.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Lost in Valdeluna (A/B map info gap) · p.3–4 Directions Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 2nd year (and exam revision). Time: about 60 minutes. All texts are original.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: Simón dice. Students stand. Simón dice: gira a la derecha… gira a la izquierda… sigue todo recto (two steps)… para. Anyone who moves without "Simón dice" sits down. Then point at the door: ¿Para ir a la cantina? and elicit what students already know.
8 minGuided discovery. Board, two columns: Sigue todo recto / Gira a la derecha / Toma la primera calle / Cruza la plaza and Siga… / Gire… / Tome… / Cruce…. Pairs answer: who would you say each column to? what happens to the last vowel? why cruce? Then drill al museo / a la estación and enfrente del cine with the poster (p.5).
15 minLost in Valdeluna (p.2). Pairs, A and B cards; don't show your map. Both start at the red dot. A asks the way to one of B's places (Perdone, ¿para ir al cine?); B gives directions with usted; A writes the place in the right dashed box. Swap after each place. Check by comparing maps at the end. Extension: add a detail (Está enfrente de…).
10 minDirections Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Answer chain below.
12 minWorksheet (p.6): read Marcos's email about Valencia, A alone, B–C in pairs. Feedback: how does Marcos get to school when it rains? (by bus)
5 minExit ticket. Write directions from the classroom to the school office (tú). Homework: play Map Dash at leim.ie/map-dash.html and copy five lines from the "Go back over these" list.

Errors to listen for: a el museo (→ al) · enfrente de el cine (→ del) · el segundo calle (→ la segunda) · Cruze (→ cruce) · mixing sigue and gire in one sentence · en pie (→ a pie). Language awareness: Irish ar dheis / ar chlé = Spanish a la derecha / a la izquierda. Culture: plaza Mayor, casco antiguo, the AVE high-speed train. Support: leave the poster up. Fast finishers: draw your own town and give a partner directions.

Domino answer chain:

  1. los semáforos = the traffic lights
  2. un billete de ida y vuelta = a return ticket
  3. Está lejos. = It's far.
  4. Está cerca. = It's near.
  5. en la esquina = on the corner
  6. Voy a pie. = I walk. (I go on foot.)
  7. la taquilla = the ticket office
  8. Sigue todo recto. = Go straight on.
  9. hasta el final de la calle = to the end of the street
  10. el andén = the platform
  11. Gira a la izquierda. = Turn left.
  12. LLEGADAS = arrivals
  13. a cinco minutos andando = five minutes' walk away
  14. Toma la segunda a la derecha. = Take the second on the right.
  15. Cruza la plaza. = Cross the square.
  16. Voy en tren. = I go by train.
  17. al lado de la iglesia = next to the church
  18. enfrente del cine = opposite the cinema
  19. ¿Hay un banco por aquí? = Is there a bank near here?
  20. la parada de autobús = the bus stop

Worksheet

Getting around Valencia

↗ Léim
¡Hola, Cian! ¡Ya falta poco para tu visita a Valencia! Te explico cómo llegar desde el aeropuerto. Es muy fácil: coge el metro (la línea 3 o la 5) y bájate en la parada de Xàtiva. El viaje dura unos veinte minutos. Al salir del metro, verás la plaza del Ayuntamiento. Yo te espero allí, enfrente de la oficina de Correos, al lado de una fuente grande. Mi piso está a diez minutos andando.
En Valencia mucha gente va en bici porque la ciudad es llana y hay muchos carriles bici. Yo voy al instituto en bici todos los días, pero cuando llueve cojo el autobús. Mis padres van al trabajo en coche y siempre se quejan del tráfico. El sábado podemos ir en bici al parque del Turia: antes era un río y ahora es un parque enorme. Luego podemos seguir hasta la playa de la Malvarrosa. ¿Cómo vas tú al instituto? ¿Hay metro en Cork? Un abrazo, Marcos

A. Answer in English

  1. How does Cian get from the airport?
  2. Where does he get off? How long is the journey?
  3. Where exactly will Marcos wait for him?
  4. How far is Marcos's flat from there?
  1. Why do many people in Valencia cycle?
  2. How does Marcos get to school? And when it rains?
  3. What do his parents complain about?
  4. What was the Turia park before? What will they do after the park?

B. Find the Spanish in the email

  1. get off at
  2. the journey takes about twenty minutes
  1. next to a big fountain
  2. ten minutes' walk away

C. Make it polite (usted)

  1. Coge el metro.
  2. Gira a la izquierda.
  1. Cruza la plaza.
  2. Sigue todo recto.

D. Now you

Reply to Marcos as Cian (50–60 words): say how you get to school and why, how your family gets around, whether there is a metro in Cork, and tell Marcos how to get from Cork airport or the train station to your house.

Answers. A: 1 By metro (line 3 or 5). 2 At Xàtiva; about twenty minutes. 3 In the plaza del Ayuntamiento, opposite the post office, next to a big fountain. 4 Ten minutes' walk. 5 The city is flat and there are lots of cycle lanes. 6 By bike; by bus when it rains. 7 The traffic. 8 A river; go on to the beach (la Malvarrosa). · B: 9 bájate en 10 el viaje dura unos veinte minutos 11 al lado de una fuente grande 12 a diez minutos andando · C: 13 Coja el metro. 14 Gire a la izquierda. 15 Cruce la plaza. 16 Siga todo recto.
Watch out: llana = flat; el carril bici = cycle lane; se quejan de = they complain about; verás = you'll see (future of ver). Cork has no metro, so students should say No, no hay metro en Cork, pero hay autobuses.
