Quadratic equations

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): AF.4, AF.5, U.6

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can check whether a value is a root, solve quadratic equations by factorising (including x² + bx = 0 and x² = k), write a quadratic equation from its roots, and form and solve quadratic equations from area, number and height problems, rejecting roots that don't fit. Higher Level: ax² + bx + c = 0 with a > 1, the formula, and "find k".

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Root Match mingle cards + Garden Swap A/B cards · p.3 Quadratic Dominoes (cut-up) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Error Hunt + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 3rd year (Higher and Ordinary). Time: about 60 minutes. All questions are original.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: guess my number. "I'm thinking of a number. I multiply it by itself and get 36." Pairs write every answer. Elicit 6 AND −6. "Now: my number times (my number − 1) is 12." (4 and −3.) Two answers is normal for an x² equation.
8 minGuided discovery. Pairs solve (x − 2)(x − 5) = 0, (x + 1)(x − 4) = 0 and (x + 3)(x + 5) = 0 ("one bracket must be 0"), then multiply each out. "Look at the roots and the equation. What do the roots add to? Multiply to?" Build the 3 steps: make it = 0, factorise, each bracket = 0.
10 minRoot Match mingle (p.2, 24 cards = 12 equation/roots pairs; one card each). Equation card? Solve it, then find the person with your roots. Roots card? Find your equation. Pairs check each root by putting it back in.
12 minGarden Swap (p.2, pairs, don't show your card). Each fact is on only ONE card. Ask your partner for what you're missing, write the equation together, then both solve and reject the root that doesn't fit. Feedback: garden x = 7, so 7 m by 10 m, perimeter 34 m; Aoife's number is 8 (−6 doesn't count: it's positive).
10 minQuadratic Dominoes (p.3), groups of 3–4. Start with START, solve the clue, find the domino with that answer on its left, and build the chain to FINISH. Two roots are written "−1 and 3".
12 minWorksheet (p.5). Error Hunt in pairs (find the mistake, name it, fix it), then the exam-style questions alone. Question 5 is Higher Level.
3 minExit ticket. Solve x² − x − 6 = 0 and check one root. (x = 3 or x = −2.) Homework: play Root Rush at leim.ie/root-rush.html and copy three lines from the "Go back over these" list.
Mingle key (equation card → roots card): 21 → 16 · 17 → 4 · 13 → 6 · 8 → 3 · 2 → 12 · 1 → 24 · 19 → 15 · 11 → 22 · 18 → 10 · 23 → 20 · 14 → 7 · 5 → 9.
Errors to look for: not making it = 0 first · a term moved without changing sign · root 3 from (x + 3) · dividing by x and losing x = 0 · one root only for x² = k · keeping a negative length. Support: a card with "the roots multiply to the last number". Fast finishers: write an equation whose roots are −4 and 7, then one whose roots are 1/2 and 3 (Higher Level).

B. Exam-style questions (show all your work)

  1. Solve x² − 3x − 28 = 0. Check one of your roots.
  2. Write a quadratic equation with roots −1 and 6, in the form x² + bx + c = 0.
  3. A rectangular pitch is x m wide and (x + 6) m long. Its area is 91 m². (a) Write an equation in x. (b) Find the width and length.
  4. A ball's height is h = 35t − 5t² metres after t seconds. (a) When is it 50 m high? (b) Why are there two answers?
  5. Higher Level: (a) solve 3x² + 10x − 8 = 0. (b) Solve x² − 2x − 5 = 0, giving your answers to 2 decimal places.

A. Error Hunt

x² + 3x = 18 → x² + 3x + 18 = 018 changes sign when it moves: x² + 3x − 18 = 0 → (x + 6)(x − 3) = 0, x = −6 or x = 3
x² − x − 20 = 0 → (x − 5)(x + 4) = 0The sign in the bracket is the opposite of the root: x = 5 or x = −4
x² = 8x → divide by x → x = 8Dividing by x loses a root: x(x − 8) = 0, x = 0 or x = 8
x² − 100 = 0 → x² = 100Two square roots: x = 10 or x = −10
x² + 7x + 12 = 0 → (x + 6)(x + 2) = 06 + 2 = 8, not 7. (x + 3)(x + 4) = 0, x = −3 or x = −4
Width x, length x + 2, area 48 → x = 6 or x = −8A length can't be negative: width 6 m, length 8 m

B. Exam-style questions

  1. (x − 7)(x + 4) = 0: x = 7 or x = −4. Check: 49 − 21 − 28 = 0 ✓
  2. (x + 1)(x − 6) = 0: x² − 5x − 6 = 0
  3. (a) x(x + 6) = 91 → x² + 6x − 91 = 0 (b) (x + 13)(x − 7) = 0, x = 7 (not −13): 7 m wide, 13 m long
  4. (a) 5t² − 35t + 50 = 0 → t² − 7t + 10 = 0 → (t − 2)(t − 5) = 0: t = 2 s and t = 5 s (b) once on the way up, once on the way down
  5. (a) (3x − 2)(x + 4) = 0: x = 2/3 or x = −4 (b) x = (2 ± √24) ÷ 2: x = −1.45 or x = 3.45

Marking tip

Give credit for each step, as the exam does: making it = 0, correct factors, each root. One root on its own is not finished. In word problems insist on a sentence saying which root is rejected and why, and units in the answer.
