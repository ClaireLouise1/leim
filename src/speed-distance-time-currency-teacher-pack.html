Speed, distance, time & currency

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): N.3b, GT.1, U.6 (straight-line graphs and slope come later, in coordinate geometry)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can find speed, distance and time (changing minutes into hours and back), work out arrival times and average speed for journeys with more than one part or a stop, change between km/h and m/s, read distance–time graphs, and convert between euro and other currencies, including commission and comparing prices.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Road Trip mingle cards + Journey Swap A/B cards · p.3 Travel Dominoes (cut-up) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Error Hunt + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 2nd year (Higher and Ordinary), or 3rd-year revision. Time: about 60 minutes. All questions are original.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: the 2.30 trap. Board "A coach travels 150 km in 2 h 30 min. What is its average speed?" Students answer alone on mini-whiteboards. Collect answers (usually 65.2 and 60 km/h) without saying which is right.
8 minGuided discovery. Pairs fill a table for a car at a steady speed (30 min → 50 km, 1 h → 100 km, 1 h 30 min → 150 km). What is distance ÷ time each time? What must 30 minutes be in hours? Resolve the lead-in, then build S = D ÷ T, D = S × T, T = D ÷ S. Then: Niamh drives there at 60 km/h and back at 40 km/h. Is her average 50?
10 minRoad Trip mingle (p.2, 24 cards = 12 question/answer pairs; one card each). Question card? Work it out and find the person with your answer. Answer card? Find your question. Pairs check each other's working.
12 minJourney Swap (p.2, pairs, don't show your card). Each fact is on only ONE card. Ask your partner for what you're missing ("What time do they leave?"), then both fill every blank. Feedback: arrival 12:15, average 72 km/h; euro back €96.59.
10 minTravel Dominoes (p.3), groups of 3–4. Start with START, work out the clue, find the domino with that answer on its left, and build the chain to FINISH.
12 minWorksheet (p.5). Error Hunt in pairs (find the mistake, name the rule, fix it), then the exam-style questions alone.
3 minExit ticket. 90 km in 1 h 15 min: speed? €350 at €1 = £0.86: sterling? (72 km/h; £301.) Homework: play Road Trip at leim.ie/road-trip.html and copy three lines from the "Go back over these" list.
Road Trip key: 300 km in 4 h: speed? → 75 km/h · 40 km/h for 2 h 15 min: distance? → 90 km · 240 km at 96 km/h: time? → 2 h 30 min · 1 h 12 min in hours? → 1.2 h · Leave 14:35, drive 2 h 40 min. Arrive? → 17:15 · 10 m/s in km/h? → 36 km/h · 100 km in 1 h + 50 km in 1 h 30 min: average? → 60 km/h · Graph flat 11:00 to 11:45: stopped for? → 45 min · €250 at €1 = £0.84 → £210.00 · €600 at €1 = $1.15 → $690.00 · $324 at €1 = $1.08, in euro? → €300.00 · €1,000 gave £865: €1 = £? → £0.865.
Errors to look for: 2 h 30 min as 2.3 · dividing for distance · the mean of two speeds · leaving out stops · multiplying to go back into euro · forgetting the commission · reading a distance off the graph when a speed is asked. Support: a minutes → hours strip (15 = 0.25, 20 = ⅓, 30 = 0.5, 45 = 0.75). Fast finishers: how long does light take to reach us from the Sun, 150,000,000 km at 300,000 km/s? (500 s = 8 min 20 s)

B. Exam-style questions (show all your work)

  1. A train leaves at 06:55 and travels 286 km at an average speed of 104 km/h. (a) How long does the journey take, in hours and minutes? (b) At what time does it arrive?
  2. Ronan drives 84 km in 1 h 10 min, stops for 20 minutes, then drives 126 km in 1 h 30 min. Find his average speed for the whole journey, including the stop.
  3. A bank takes 1.5% commission from the euro and changes the rest at €1 = $1.12. How many dollars do you get for €900?
  4. A train travels at 30 m/s. (a) Write this speed in km/h. (b) How long does it take to travel 27 km at this speed?
  5. A jacket costs £79.99 in Belfast and €89.50 in Cork. The rate is €1 = £0.87. Where is it cheaper, and by how much?

A. Error Hunt

150 km in 2 h 30 min. Speed? 150 ÷ 2.3 = 65.2 km/h2 h 30 min = 2.5 h. 150 ÷ 2.5 = 60 km/h
80 km/h for 45 min. Distance? 80 ÷ 0.75 = 106.7 kmDistance = speed × time: 80 × 0.75 = 60 km
1.25 hours in hours and minutes? 1 h 25 min0.25 × 60 = 15 min, so 1 h 15 min
60 km at 30 km/h, back at 60 km/h. Average speed? (30 + 60) ÷ 2 = 45 km/h120 km in 2 h + 1 h = 3 h: 40 km/h
Change £258 into euro at €1 = £0.86. 258 × 0.86 = €221.88Into euro: divide. 258 ÷ 0.86 = €300.00
A graph is flat at 20 km from 10:00 to 10:30. Speed? 20 km/hFlat = not moving: the speed is 0 km/h (stopped for 30 min)

B. Exam-style questions

  1. (a) 286 ÷ 104 = 2.75 h = 2 h 45 min (b) 06:55 + 2 h 45 min = 09:40
  2. 210 km in 1 h 10 min + 20 min + 1 h 30 min = 3 h: 70 km/h
  3. €900 − 1.5% = €886.50; × 1.12 = $992.88
  4. (a) 30 × 3.6 = 108 km/h (b) 27 ÷ 108 = 0.25 h = 15 min
  5. £79.99 ÷ 0.87 = €91.94. Cork is cheaper by €2.44

Marking tip

Give credit for each step, as the exam does: the time in hours, the formula, the substitution and the answer with units each earn marks. For currency, credit the correct operation (× or ÷) even if the rounding slips. For "explain" parts insist on a sentence with numbers in it.
