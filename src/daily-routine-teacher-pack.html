Junior Cycle Spanish · Teacher pack

Daily routine & reflexive verbs

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 1.20, 2.1 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 1.15, 2.1 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can describe a typical day with reflexive verbs in all persons (me levanto, ¿a qué hora te acuestas?, mi hermano se ducha), use the stem changers despertarse, acostarse, vestirse, dormirse, put the day in order with times and sequencers (primero, luego, después, por último), and contrast school days with weekends.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Early Bird info gap (two cards) · p.3–4 Routine Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 1st–3rd year. Time: about 60 minutes.

Procedure

5 minLead-in. Mime your morning (alarm, stretch, shower, teeth…) while saying nothing. Students call out the English; you recast in Spanish: Sí, me despierto… me levanto… Write the six verbs up in order.
10 minGuided discovery. Board: Me levanto a las siete. / ¿A qué hora te levantas? / Mi hermano se levanta a las ocho. Ask: "What's the same? What changes apart from the ending?" Elicit me, te, se, nos, os, se and that the dictionary form ends in -se. Then me despierto / nos despertamos: "Where does the stem change, where not?" (the boot). Concept checks: "Lavo el coche or me lavo el coche?" "Me lavo mis dientes: right or wrong?" "Me gusta levantar…: what goes on the end?"
5 minPoster (p.5). Drill the six persons of levantarse as a chant, then the four stem changers and the sequencers.
15 minEarly Bird (p.2), pairs A and B. A is Hugo, B is Nerea. They ask the eight tú questions, write the partner's answers, then write three comparisons in the third person (Hugo se levanta a las siete menos cuarto, pero Nerea se levanta a las siete y media). Who is the early bird? (Hugo.) Fast pairs ask each other the same questions for real.
10 minRoutine Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Deal out the dominoes; players match a Spanish phrase to its English. First to place all their dominoes wins. Answer chain below.
10 minWorksheet (p.6) A and B in pairs. Feed back on the error correction.
5 minExit ticket. Three sentences about tomorrow morning in order (Primero… luego… por último…). Homework: play Routine Order-Up at leim.ie/routine-order-up.html and copy one finished day from the game into your copy.
Errors to listen for: Yo se levanto (me) · pronoun left out: Levanto a las siete (me levanto) · me desperto / me acosto / me vesto (stem change) · nos acuestamos (no change for nosotros) · Me lavo mis dientes (los dientes) · Me gusta me levantar (levantarme) · Antes de acostarse, leo (acostarme: it matches the person) · Después de ceno (de + infinitive: cenar).
Culture note: in Spain breakfast is often light (toast with tomato and oil, or biscuits and milk), many secondary schools run from about 8:30 to 2:30, la comida at 2–3 pm is the main meal, la merienda is an afternoon snack, and dinner is at 9–10 pm. Support: give weaker students the poster as a mat. Fast finishers: write Hugo's and Nerea's day as a short paragraph in the third person.

Worksheet

Valeria's day in Bilbao

↗ Léim

A. Read Valeria's email

¡Hola, Niamh! ¿Qué tal? En tu último correo me preguntaste por mi rutina, así que aquí va. Vivo en Bilbao y voy a un instituto en el centro. Me despierto a las siete, pero me quedo en la cama diez minutos más con el móvil (¡mi madre se enfada!). Luego me ducho rápido, me visto y me peino. Mi hermano Iker tarda muchísimo en el baño porque se afeita y se pone gomina en el pelo. Desayuno un café con leche y una tostada con aceite. Voy al instituto en metro con mi amiga Ane; el viaje dura veinte minutos. Las clases empiezan a las ocho y cuarto. A las once tenemos el recreo y como un bocadillo. Vuelvo a casa a las tres y cuarto y como con mis abuelos, que viven con nosotros. Por la tarde hago los deberes, y los lunes y los miércoles voy a clase de guitarra. Cenamos a las nueve y después de cenar me relajo: escucho música o hablo con mis amigas. Me acuesto a las once, pero normalmente no me duermo hasta las doce. Los domingos no me levanto hasta las doce y comemos paella en casa de mis tíos. ¿Y tú? ¿A qué hora te levantas? ¿Qué desayunas en Irlanda? Un abrazo, Valeria
  1. What does Valeria do for ten minutes after she wakes up, and how does her mum react?
  2. What three things does she do before breakfast?
  3. Why does Iker take so long in the bathroom?
  4. What does she have for breakfast?
  5. How does she get to school, who with, and how long does it take?
  6. When do classes start?
  7. What happens at eleven?
  8. Who does she have lunch with, and why?
  9. What does she do on Mondays and Wednesdays?
  10. What time does she go to bed, and when does she usually fall asleep?

B. Find and fix the mistake

  1. Yo te levanto a las siete.
  2. Mi hermano se afeito todos los días.
  3. Me lavo mis dientes.
  4. Nosotros nos acuestamos tarde.
  1. Mi madre se veste muy rápido.
  2. Tengo que levantarse temprano.
  3. Después de ceno, veo la tele.
  4. Mis padres se despierta a las seis.

C. Now you

Write Niamh's reply to Valeria (50–70 words). Answer her two questions, describe your school morning in order, and say how your Sundays are different.

Answers. A: 1 She stays in bed with her phone; her mum gets annoyed. 2 She has a quick shower, gets dressed and brushes her hair. 3 He shaves and puts gel in his hair. 4 A white coffee and toast with (olive) oil. 5 By metro, with her friend Ane; 20 minutes. 6 At 8:15. 7 Break: she eats a sandwich. 8 Her grandparents, because they live with the family. 9 Guitar class. 10 At eleven; not until twelve. · B: 11 me levanto 12 se afeita 13 los dientes 14 nos acostamos 15 se viste 16 levantarme 17 después de cenar 18 se despiertan.
Watch out: me quedo en la cama = I stay in bed; se enfada = gets annoyed; tarda muchísimo = takes ages; la gomina = hair gel; el recreo = break.
