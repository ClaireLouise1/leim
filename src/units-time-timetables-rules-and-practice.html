Units, time & timetables

1. Metric units

MeasureUnits
Length1 km = 1000 m · 1 m = 100 cm · 1 cm = 10 mm
Mass1 kg = 1000 g · 1 tonne = 1000 kg
Capacity1 litre = 1000 ml

Big → small: multiply (3.4 km = 3400 m). Small → big: divide (250 g = 0.25 kg). Change to the same unit before adding, comparing or dividing: 1.2 kg + 350 g = 1200 g + 350 g = 1550 g.

2. The 24-hour clock

am: keep the hour (7:05 am = 07:05; 12:40 am = 00:40). pm: add 12 (2:35 pm = 14:35), but 12:30 pm = 12:30. Back again: 13–23, take away 12 and write pm (19:05 = 7:05 pm).

3. Hours are not decimals

An hour has 60 minutes. Minutes → hours: ÷ 60 (45 min = 0.75 h). Decimal hours → minutes: × 60 (0.4 h = 24 min, so 2.4 h = 2 h 24 min, not 2 h 40 min).

4. How long is it? Count on

08:47 → 11:15: 08:47 → 09:00 = 13 min; 09:00 → 11:00 = 2 h; 11:00 → 11:15 = 15 min. Total 2 h 28 min. Don't take away like ordinary numbers (1115 − 847 = 268 gives "2 h 68 min", which can't be right).

5. Adding time

13:50 + 2 h 35 min: add the hours (15:50), then the minutes (15:50 + 35 min = 16:25). Every 60 minutes makes another hour.

6. Reading a timetable

Each column is one bus; each row is a stop. Read down to follow one bus. A dash (—) means it doesn't stop there. For "the latest bus to get there by…", find the last arrival that's early enough, then go up the column.

Other units of time: 60 seconds = 1 minute · 24 hours = 1 day · 7 days = 1 week · 365 days = 1 year (366 in a leap year).
Exam tip: always write the unit in your answer (450 g, not 450). When a question mixes units, change everything to the smaller unit first. Check: a smaller unit means a bigger number.

  1. A train leaves Cork at 06:55 and arrives in Dublin Heuston at 09:28. (a) How long is the journey? (b) One morning it is 17 minutes late. When does it arrive?
  2. A wall needs 3.6 litres of paint. Paint comes in 750 ml tins. (a) How many tins must be bought? (b) How much paint is left over?
  3. Niamh swims 48 lengths of a 25 m pool in 36 minutes. (a) How far does she swim, in km? (b) Write 36 minutes in hours, as a decimal.
  4. A ham needs 40 minutes per kg plus 20 minutes. It weighs 2.25 kg. Dinner is at 19:00. What is the latest time it can go in the oven?
