Exam skills: Listening & Reading

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 1.1, 1.3, 1.6, 1.7, 2.5, 2.6, 2.7 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 1.3, 1.5, 1.6, 1.17, 2.4, 2.7, 2.9 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can tell a gist question from a detail question and read or listen accordingly, predict the kind of answer from the question word, use cognates to understand new words, avoid common false friends (embarazada, la librería, constipado), decode number, time, price and date traps, and catch "change of mind" answers after pero, al final, perdón.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Read & Report info gap (two cards) · p.3–4 False Friend Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 1st–3rd year. Time: about 60 minutes. All texts are original: no exam material is reproduced.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: two questions. Show a short notice (Excursión a Madrid el jueves doce de marzo. El autobús sale a las ocho…) and two questions: What is this about? / When does the bus leave? Pairs decide: which one needs the whole text, which needs three words? Elicit gist and detail.
8 minGuided discovery: friends and false friends. Write 8 words on the board (la estación, famoso, rápidamente, la ciudad, embarazada, la librería, la carpeta, el éxito). Pairs guess the meaning, then spot the endings pattern (-ción, -oso, -mente, -dad). Reveal the four false friends with the poster (p.5).
15 minRead & Report (p.2), pairs A and B. Each reads their text aloud twice; the partner answers in English. First listen for gist, second for detail. Then check and do Step 3 (trap sentences). Answers below.
10 minFalse Friend Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Match each Spanish word to its real meaning. Includes four number and time traps. Answer chain below.
12 minWorksheet (p.6). A alone in 3 minutes (gist only: tick a title), B–C in pairs, D alone. Feedback: for each answer, students point to the Spanish words that gave it away.
5 minExit ticket. Write one gist and one detail question about today's worksheet text. Homework: play Gist or Detail? at leim.ie/gist-or-detail.html and copy five lines from the "Go back over these" list.
Read & Report answers. A (about B's pool): 1 a (municipal) swimming pool 2 10 am to 9 pm 3 €2 4 Tuesday 5 a swimming cap 6 a 10-swim card for €30. B (about A's cinema): 1 a cinema 2 an adventure film 3 6:00 and 8:30 4 €5 5 popcorn, for under-12s 6 Sunday.
Errors to listen for: menos cuarto read as "quarter past" · catorce / cuatro / cuarenta and sesenta / setenta mixed up · answers copied in Spanish · one detail given where two are asked · the first answer heard instead of the final one. Language awareness: link to Irish cluastuiscint and English comprehension: same strategy, questions first. Support: weaker students can read their partner's text after the second listen. Fast finishers: write a third card with a new notice.

Worksheet

Javier's exchange in Kerry

↗ Léim

A. Gist: read in 1 minute and tick the best title

My exchange in Kerry   My school in Bilbao   How to learn English   A football match report

¡Hola! Soy Javier, tengo quince años y soy de Bilbao. Este mes estoy de intercambio en Killarney, en el condado de Kerry, con la familia de Cian. Al principio estaba muy nervioso porque mi inglés no es perfecto, pero la familia es muy simpática. Cian tiene dos hermanos: Aoife, de diecisiete años, y Rory, que tiene solo cuatro. Las clases empiezan a las nueve menos diez y terminan a las cuatro menos veinte. Lo que más me gusta es el campo: el martes hicimos senderismo en el Parque Nacional. Íbamos a subir a una montaña, pero había mucha niebla, así que al final visitamos una casa antigua. El sábado vamos a asistir a un partido de fútbol gaélico. Vuelvo a Bilbao el veintiocho de abril. ¡Voy a echar de menos Irlanda!

B. Detail: answer in English

  1. Why was Javier nervous at first?
  2. How old is Rory?
  3. When do classes start and finish?
  4. What does he like most?
  1. What did they plan to do on Tuesday, what did they do in the end, and why?
  2. What are they going to do on Saturday?
  3. When does he go back to Bilbao?
  4. How does he feel about leaving?

C. Friends and false friends: find in the text

  1. a cognate for "nervous"
  2. a cognate for "National Park"
  3. el campo means
  1. asistir a un partido means
  2. write in figures: diecisiete
  3. write in figures: las nueve menos diez

D. Now you

Write a short notice in Spanish (30–40 words) about a school trip or event. Then write one gist and three detail questions about it in English. Swap with a partner.

Answers. A: My exchange in Kerry. · B: 2 His English isn't perfect. 3 Four. 4 Start 8:50, finish 3:40. 5 The countryside. 6 Planned to climb a mountain; in the end visited an old house; it was very foggy. 7 Go to (watch) a Gaelic football match. 8 28 April. 9 He's going to miss Ireland. · C: 10 nervioso 11 Parque Nacional 12 the countryside 13 to attend / go to a match 14 17 15 8:50.
Watch out: las cuatro menos veinte = 3:40 (not 4:20); el campo = countryside (not camp); asistir = to attend (not to assist); echar de menos = to miss.
