Learning outcome (2018 specification): GT.3a, constructions 1–15 (3 and 7 are Higher level only). Play the game: leim.ie/construction-steps.html

1. The tools

Compass: arcs and circles, copies a length · straight edge: straight lines, no measuring · ruler: measures · protractor: angles · set square: right angles and parallel lines.

Constructions 1 and 2 (angle bisector, perpendicular bisector): compass and straight edge only.

2. Golden rules

Sharp pencil · leave every arc showing (it's your proof) · label every point · set the compass against the ruler from 0 · keep the same width when the step says so · sketch triangles first with their measurements.

3. The 15 constructions

1. Bisector of an angle
Arc from the vertex across both arms; equal arcs from those two points; join the vertex to where they cross.
2. Perpendicular bisector of a segment
Compass more than half of |AB|; equal arcs from A and B above and below; join the crossings.
3. Perpendicular from a point not on l (HL)
Arc from P cutting l twice; equal arcs from those points on the other side; join P to the crossing.
4. Perpendicular at a point on l
Arcs from P cutting l either side; wider equal arcs from those points; join P to the crossing.
5. Parallel line through a point
Line through P crossing l; copy the angle at the crossing up to P with arcs; join P to the new point.
6. 2 or 3 equal parts
Ray from A; step off equal marks; join the last mark to B; parallels through the other marks.
7. Any number of equal parts (HL)
Same as 6, with as many marks as parts.
8. Segment of a given length on a ray
Set the compass to the length on a ruler; arc from the start of the ray.
9. Angle of a given size on a ray
Protractor centre on A, base along the ray; dot at the angle (scale from 0°); draw the ray.
10. Triangle: SSS
Draw one side; arcs from each end with the other two lengths; join up.
11. Triangle: SAS
Draw a side; draw the angle at one end; mark the second side with the compass; join up.
12. Triangle: ASA
Draw the side; draw both angles on the same side of it; they meet at the third corner.
13. Right-angled: hypotenuse + side (RHS)
Draw the side; right angle at one end; arc of the hypotenuse from the other end; join up.
14. Right-angled: side + acute angle
Draw the side; right angle at one end; the given angle at the other; they meet.
15. Rectangle
Draw one side; perpendiculars at both ends; mark the other side's length on each; join.

4. Triangles

SSS three sides · SAS two sides and the angle between them · ASA two angles and the side between them · RHS right angle, hypotenuse, side. Can it be a triangle? The two shorter sides must add to more than the longest.

Exam tip: the arcs are your working. A construction with the arcs rubbed out looks measured, and accuracy matters: lengths and angles are checked against the true values.

  2. B Draw a line through P that crosses l. Call the crossing point Q.
