1. Spot the pattern: two questions

Aviso: Instituto San Jorge: excursión a Madrid el jueves doce de marzo. El autobús sale del instituto a las ocho en punto. Precio: veinticinco euros, comida incluida.
Q1 What is this notice about?   Q2 At what time does the bus leave?

Q1 needs the whole text: it's a gist question (a school trip). Q2 needs three words (a las ocho): it's a detail question. Knowing which kind you have tells you how to read or listen.

2. Gist or detail?

Gist: the big pictureDetail: one fact
It asksWhat is it about? What kind of text? Where would you hear it? How does the writer feel overall?When? Where? How much? How many? Who? Why? Name two things…
You…skim: title, first and last lines, words you know, the tonescan for one kind of word (a time, a price, a day) and stop there
Cluesthe text type, pictures, Querido…, Atención, señores pasajerosquestion words: ¿Cuándo? ¿Dónde? ¿Quién? ¿Por qué? ¿Cuánto? ¿A qué hora?

3. Before, during and after

  1. Before: read the questions first. Underline the question word. Predict the kind of answer: a time? a price? a place? a reason?
  2. During: first time through, the gist. Next time, the details. If you hear a piece more than once, give each listen a different job. Unknown words? Keep going.
  3. After: does your answer match the question word? Did you give the number of points asked (two things)? Is it sensible (€550 for a T-shirt?)? Never leave a blank.

4. Cognates: free words

-ción → -tionla estación, la información
-dad → -tyla ciudad, la universidad
-oso → -ousfamoso, delicioso, nervioso
-mente → -lyrápidamente, finalmente
es- → s-la escuela, el estudiante, el estómago

5. False friends

embarazada = pregnantnot embarrassed
la librería = bookshoplibrary = la biblioteca
constipado = has a coldnot constipated
el éxito = successexit = la salida
la carpeta = folder · la ropa = clothesnot carpet · not rope
largo = long · recordar = to remembernot large · not record

6. Traps examiners love

Numbers: cuatro 4 · catorce 14 · cuarenta 40 · seis 6 · dieciséis 16 · sesenta 60 · setenta 70 · quince 15 · cincuenta 50 · doce 12 · dos 2.
Times: las nueve menos cuarto = 8:45 (not 9:45) · y media = half past. Prices: doce euros con cincuenta = €12.50.
Days and months: martes (Tue) / miércoles (Wed) · junio (June) / julio (July).
Change of mind: the first thing you hear is often not the answer. Listen for pero, sin embargo, al final, ahora, perdón: El partido no es el sábado; es el domingo. → Sunday.

Irish and English link: the same skills as your Irish cluastuiscint and léamhthuiscint and English comprehension: questions first, key word, check.
Exam tip: Listening (140 marks) and Reading (140 marks) are 280 of the 360 marks on the JC Spanish paper. Answer in English when the question is in English, don't copy chunks of Spanish, and give exactly what's asked.
