Expanding & simplifying

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): AF.3a, AF.3b (factorising comes later in Algebra 3, AF.3d; dividing and algebraic fractions in Algebra 4, AF.3a/AF.3c)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can multiply terms; expand a single bracket (including a negative or a letter outside); expand and simplify sums and differences of brackets; expand double brackets with the area model; square a bracket; and recognise the difference of two squares. Higher Level: a bracket with three terms.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Bracket Relay + Area Model Match cards · p.3 Expanding Dominoes (cut-up) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Error Hunt + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 2nd year (Higher and Ordinary), or 3rd-year revision. Time: about 60 minutes. All questions are original.

Procedure

6 minLead-in: 23 × 14 without a calculator. Draw a rectangle split into (20 + 3) by (10 + 4): 200 + 80 + 30 + 12 = 322. Then swap 20 for x: (x + 3)(x + 4). Pairs fill the four boxes: x² + 4x + 3x + 12 = x² + 7x + 12.
8 minGuided discovery. Pairs expand (x + 2)(x + 3), (x + 4)(x + 1), (x + 5)(x + 2), (x + 6)(x − 2) with the area model. Where do the middle and last numbers come from? (Added; multiplied.) Then test the claim "(x + 3)² = x² + 9" with x = 1 and draw its area model: which boxes are missing?
10 minArea Model Match (p.2, 24 cards = 12 pairs; one card each). Picture and bracket cards find their expansion. Pairs check by putting x = 1 into both cards. Feedback: why is (x + 6)(x − 6) only two terms?
10 minBracket Relay (p.2), teams of 4 in a line. Runner 1 does step 1 and passes the answer (not the card) to runner 2, and so on. The last runner brings the final number to you: if it's wrong, the team finds the broken step.
10 minExpanding Dominoes (p.3), groups of 3–4: start with START, work out the clue, find the domino with that answer on its left and build the chain to FINISH.
13 minWorksheet (p.5). Error Hunt in pairs (find the mistake, name the rule, fix it), then the exam-style questions alone.
3 minExit ticket. Expand and simplify 2(x − 3) − (x − 5); expand (x − 6)² (x − 1; x² − 12x + 36). Homework: play Bracket Blaster at leim.ie/bracket-blaster.html (Spot the slip round) and copy three lines from the "Go back over these" list.
Area Model Match key: picture (x + 4)(x + 2) → x² + 6x + 8 · picture (x − 3)(x + 5) → x² + 2x − 15 · picture (2x + 1)(x + 3) → 2x² + 7x + 3 · picture (x + 6)(x − 6) → x² − 36 · picture (x − 2)(x − 2) → x² − 4x + 4 · picture (3)(x − 7) → 3x − 21 · (x + 5)(x + 1) → x² + 6x + 5 · (x − 4)(x − 3) → x² − 7x + 12 · (x + 7)(x − 2) → x² + 5x − 14 · (x + 3)² → x² + 6x + 9 · x(x − 8) → x² − 8x · −2(x − 5) → −2x + 10.
Errors to look for: 3(x + 4) = 3x + 4 · −2(x − 5) = −2x − 10 · −(x − 6) = −x − 6 · x(x + 3) = x² + 3 · 2x × 3x = 6x · (x + 3)² = x² + 9 · (x + 3)(x − 4) with +12 at the end · collecting x² with x. Support: draw the area model every time; check with x = 1. Fast finishers: (x + 1)(x² + 3x − 2), or explain why (n + 1)² − n² is always odd.

Answers

Bracket Relay

  1. Relay A: x² + 5x + 6 → 4x + 6 → 11x → −33 (11(−3) = −33)
  2. Relay B: x² − 8x + 16 → 3x² + 16 → 2x² + 32 → 40 (2(4) + 32 = 40)

A. Error Hunt

Expand 4(x + 3) = 4x + 3The 4 multiplies both terms: 4x + 12
Expand −3(x − 2) = −3x − 6−3 × −2 = +6: −3x + 6
Expand (x + 5)² = x² + 25(x + 5)(x + 5) has middle terms: x² + 10x + 25
Expand x(x + 2) = x² + 2x × 2 = 2x: x² + 2x
Expand (x + 3)(x − 4) = x² − x + 123 × −4 = −12: x² − x − 12
2x × 3x = 6xx × x = x²: 6x²

B. Exam-style questions

  1. 6x − 15 − 4x + 12 = 2x − 3 (−4 × −3 = +12)
  2. x² + 2x − 7x − 14 = x² − 5x − 14
  3. (a) (2x − 3)(2x − 3) = 4x² − 6x − 6x + 9 = 4x² − 12x + 9 (b) (2x + 3)² = 4x² + 12x + 9; 4x² − 12x + 9 − 4x² − 12x − 9 = −24x
  4. (a) (x + 5)² − x² = x² + 10x + 25 − x² = 10x + 25 (b) 10(3) + 25 = 55 cm²; check: 8 × 8 − 3 × 3 = 64 − 9 = 55 ✓
  5. x³ + 3x² − 2x + x² + 3x − 2 = x³ + 4x² + x − 2

Marking tip

Give credit for each step, as the exam does: the line with all four terms of a double bracket earns marks even if the collecting slips. Accept the terms in any order (−2x + 10 = 10 − 2x), but the answer must have no brackets and no like terms left.
