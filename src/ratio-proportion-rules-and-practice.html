1. What a ratio means

"Girls to boys is 3 : 4" means 3 girls for every 4 boys. Order matters: boys to girls is 4 : 3. Simplify by dividing every number by the HCF: 12 : 18 = 2 : 3 (÷ 6). Same units first: 50 cm : 2 m = 50 : 200 = 1 : 4. Equivalent ratios: multiply both by the same number (3 : 5 = 12 : 20). Adding the same number does NOT work.

2. Sharing in a ratio

Share €60 in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. Add the parts: 1 + 2 + 3 = 6. One part = €60 ÷ 6 = €10. Shares: €10, €20, €30. Check they add up to €60.

Each share is a fraction of the total: Tara gets 3/6 = 1/2. In 2 : 3, the first person gets 2/5 (the bottom is the total number of parts).

3. One share or the difference known

Red : blue = 3 : 5 and there are 21 red. 3 parts = 21, so 1 part = 7 and blue = 5 × 7 = 35. Ratio 2 : 7, one gets €35 more: 7 − 2 = 5 parts = €35, 1 part = €7, total 9 × 7 = €63.

4. Proportion: the unitary method

Find one first, then multiply. 4 tickets cost €18. 1 costs €18 ÷ 4 = €4.50, so 7 cost €31.50. Recipe for 6 uses 300 g → 50 g each → 9 people need 450 g (not 303 g).

100 cm = 1 m · 1 000 m = 1 km · so 100 000 cm = 1 km

6. Best buy

Find the price of one (or of 100 g) in each pack. 6 for €4.20 = 70c each; 10 for €6.50 = 65c each, so the 10-pack is better value.

7. Absolute v relative

Absolute = how many or how much (Class B had 15 passes, Class A 12). Relative = as a fraction or % of the whole (A: 12/20 = 60%, B: 15/30 = 50%), so A did better relatively. Relative change = change ÷ start.

Exam tip: write the "1 part" step (€840 ÷ 12 = €70): it earns marks even if a later line slips. Check your shares add to the total. Convert to the same units, and give the answer in the units asked for.
