Junior Cycle Spanish · Teacher pack

Weather & seasons

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 1.1, 1.3, 3.1 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 1.3, 1.4, 3.1 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can describe the weather with hace, está, hay, llueve, nieva (hace sol, está nublado, hay niebla), give temperatures (hace 25 grados, 3 grados bajo cero), name the seasons and say what the weather is like in each, say what they do cuando llueve / cuando hace sol, and recognise yesterday / today / tomorrow forms and forecast words (llovió, va a llover, lloverá, habrá).

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Weather Map info gap (two cards) · p.3–4 Weather Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 1st–3rd year. Time: about 60 minutes.

Procedure

5 minLead-in. Look out of the window: ¿Qué tiempo hace hoy? Students guess in English, you reformulate in Spanish on the board. Mime five weathers (shiver, fan yourself, umbrella…); students call out the Spanish.
10 minGuided discovery. Board: Hace sol. Hace viento. Está nublado. Hay niebla. Llueve. Nieva. Ask: "In English these all start with it's. What are the four different patterns? What type of word comes after each?" Elicit hace + noun, está + adjective, hay + noun, llueve / nieva alone. Concept checks: "Hace nublado or está nublado?" "Hace llueve: right or wrong?" "Muy calor or mucho calor?"
5 minPoster (p.5). Drill the symbols, the seasons and the yesterday / today / tomorrow row. Point out the forecast forms lloverá, habrá for reading.
15 minWeather Map (p.2), pairs A and B. Each card shows the forecast for some cities; the other cities are blank. Students ask ¿Qué tiempo hace en Bilbao? and answer in full sentences (En Bilbao llueve y hace doce grados), drawing the symbol and writing the temperature on their map. Then they answer the Step 2 questions together. Check: the hottest city is Sevilla (31°), the coldest is Zaragoza (−3°).
10 minWeather Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Deal out the dominoes; players match a Spanish phrase to its English. First to place all their dominoes wins. Answer chain below.
10 minWorksheet (p.6) A, B and C in pairs. Feed back on the error correction.
5 minExit ticket. Three sentences: today's weather, your favourite season and why, what you do when it rains. Homework: play Forecast Match at leim.ie/forecast-match.html and beat your score in Exam mix.
Errors to listen for: Hace nublado (está nublado) · Hace llueve / hace nieve (llueve / nieva) · Es frío (hace frío) · Hace muy calor (mucho calor) · Hay sol (hace sol) · nieve (snow, noun) vs nieva (it snows) · Ayer hace sol (hizo) · Mañana va a llueve (va a llover) · capital letters on months and seasons.
Culture note: Spain's climate varies a lot: the green, rainy north (Galicia, Asturias, the Basque Country) is the closest to Ireland; Andalucía regularly passes 40 degrees in summer; Madrid has cold winters and hot summers, and there is skiing in the Pyrenees and the Sierra Nevada. In the southern hemisphere (Argentina, Chile) the seasons are reversed, so Christmas is in summer. Support: give weaker students the poster as a mat. Fast finishers: write a TV forecast for Ireland and present it.

B. Read Valentina's email

¡Hola, Niamh! Te escribo desde Buenos Aires, en Argentina. Aquí ahora es verano y hace mucho calor: ayer hizo treinta y cinco grados. ¡Qué raro para ti! En diciembre vosotros tenéis invierno, ¿no? Nosotros celebramos la Navidad en verano: comemos en el jardín y por la tarde vamos a la piscina. En enero mi familia va a ir de vacaciones a la costa, a Mar del Plata, donde hace sol casi todos los días. Mi estación favorita es el otoño porque no hace ni frío ni calor, y los árboles de mi barrio son de color naranja. En invierno, en julio, hace frío, pero casi nunca nieva en Buenos Aires. Para ver la nieve vamos a Bariloche, en el sur, y allí esquiamos. ¿Qué tiempo hace en Irlanda en Navidad? ¿Nieva? Un abrazo, Valentina
  1. What season is it in Buenos Aires now, and what's the weather like?
  2. What was the temperature yesterday?
  3. How does her family spend Christmas Day?
  4. Where are they going in January, and what's the weather like there?
  5. Which is her favourite season? Give two reasons.
  6. Which month is winter in Argentina? Does it snow in Buenos Aires?
  7. Where does the family go to see snow, and what do they do there?
  8. What two questions does Valentina ask?

C. Find and fix the mistake

  1. Hoy hace nublado.
  2. En Irlanda hace llueve mucho.
  3. En invierno hace muy frío.
  4. Hay sol en Sevilla.
  1. Ayer hace mucho viento.
  2. Mañana va a nieva.
  3. Mi estación favorita es Verano.
  4. Es frío en enero.

D. Now you

Write Niamh's reply to Valentina (50–70 words). Say what the weather is like in Ireland at Christmas and in two other seasons, your favourite season and why, and what you do when it rains.
