Money: bills, VAT, profit & loss

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): N.2c, U.6. Play the game: leim.ie/shop-keeper.html

1. Receipts and bills

Multiply each line first (3 × €1.25 = €3.75), then add the lines. Change = what you paid − the total. Phone plans: monthly charge + only the extras over the allowance. Call-out charges: fixed charge + hourly rate × hours.

2. Electricity and gas bills

Units usedpresent − previous = 24 015 − 23 415 = 600
Cost of units600 × €0.28 = €168.00
+ standing charge€168.00 + €19.50 = €187.50
+ VAT at 9%€187.50 × 1.09 = €204.375 → €204.38

The standing charge is a fixed amount you pay whatever you use.

3. VAT

VAT is a tax added to the price. In Ireland the standard rate is 23%; lower rates include 13.5% and 9%. The question always tells you the rate.

Add VAT: €400 × 1.23 = €492. Before VAT: €492 is 123%, so €492 ÷ 1.23 = €400. Never take 23% off €492 (that gives €378.84).

4. Discounts and sales

25% off €80 → €80 × 0.75 = €60. Price before the sale: €60 ÷ 0.75 = €80.

5. Profit and loss

Profit = selling price − cost price (negative = a loss).

% profit on the cost price = profit ÷ cost × 100. Bought €40, sold €50: 10 ÷ 40 × 100 = 25%.

Selling price: cost €40 at 25% profit → €40 × 1.25 = €50. Back to the cost: €50 ÷ 1.25 = €40.

6. Mark-up and margin Higher Level

Profit as a % of…€60 → €75
Mark-upthe cost price15 ÷ 60 = 25%
Marginthe selling price15 ÷ 75 = 20%

The margin is always the smaller one. Margin 40% on a cost of €54: cost = 60% of the selling price, so €54 ÷ 0.6 = €90.

7. Value for money

Compare the price of the same amount: per 100 g, per litre or per item. 500 g for €3.40 = 68c per 100 g; 1.25 kg for €8 = 64c per 100 g, so the big bag is better value. "3 for the price of 2" = 33⅓% off. For plans, work out each total for this person.

Exam tip: write every line with € signs (units used, cost, standing charge, subtotal, VAT, total): each step earns marks. Give money to the nearest cent. Going backwards? Divide by 1.23, 0.75 or 1.25.

A. Bills and receipts

  1. Receipt: 3 × €1.45, 2 × €2.80 and 1 × €4.99. What is the total?
  2. You pay for the shopping in question 1 with a €20 note. How much change do you get?
  3. Electricity: previous reading 23 415, present reading 24 015. Units cost 28c and the standing charge is €19.50. Find the bill before VAT.
  4. Add VAT at 9% to the bill in question 3. Give the total to the nearest cent.
  5. A phone plan costs €20 a month and includes 200 minutes. Extra minutes cost 12c each. Ciarán uses 265 minutes. Find his bill.
  6. A plumber charges a call-out charge of €70 plus €48 an hour. The job takes 2.5 hours. Find the bill.

B. VAT and discounts

  1. A bike costs €80 before VAT. Find the price including VAT at 23%.
  2. Find the VAT at 13.5% on a hotel bill of €250.
  3. A TV costs €615 including VAT at 23%. Find the price before VAT.
  4. A meal costs €218 including VAT at 9%. How much of that is VAT?
  5. A €90 jacket is reduced by 20%. Find the sale price.
  6. A lamp costs €45 after 25% off. What was the price before the sale?
  7. What number do you multiply by to add VAT at 13.5%?
  8. A €400 TV has VAT at 23% added, then 10% is taken off the total in a sale. Find the sale price.

C. Profit and loss

  1. A shop buys a hurley for €35 and sells it for €49. Find the profit.
  2. Bought for €40, sold for €50. Find the % profit on the cost price.
  3. Bought for €80, sold for €68. Find the % loss on the cost price.
  4. A phone costs a shop €120. It is sold at a profit of 30% on the cost price. Find the selling price.
  5. A car cost €2,500 and is sold at a loss of 12%. Find the selling price.
  6. A shop sells a jacket for €91, making 30% profit on the cost price. Find the cost price.
  7. Higher Level: Bought for €16, sold for €20. Find the mark-up.
  8. Higher Level: Bought for €16, sold for €20. Find the margin.

D. Value for money

  1. A 750 g box of cereal costs €3.60. Find the cost of 100 g.
  2. Which is better value: 400 g for €2.20 or 1 kg for €5.20?
  3. You need 3 notebooks at €4 each. Which is cheaper: "3 for the price of 2" or "30% off each"?
  4. A 500 ml bottle costs €1.10 and a 2 litre bottle costs €3.80. How much do you save buying one 2 litre bottle instead of four 500 ml bottles?
  5. Gym A: €30 a month. Gym B: €8 a month plus €3.50 a visit. Éabha goes 7 times a month. Which is cheaper, and by how much?
  6. A bus fare is €2.40 each way. A weekly ticket is €21. How much do you save over 5 school days (there and back) with the weekly ticket?
  7. A pack of 6 yoghurts costs €4.50. One yoghurt on its own costs 80c. How much cheaper is each yoghurt in the pack?
  8. Higher Level: A shop buys a lamp for €54 and wants a margin of 40%. Find the selling price.

E. Exam-style questions (show your work)

  1. The electricity bill. The meter read 61 052 at the start of the period and 61 902 at the end. Units cost 33c each and the standing charge is €42.30. VAT at 9% is added to the whole bill. (a) How many units were used? (b) Find the bill before VAT. (c) Find the total bill, to the nearest cent.
  2. The candle stall. Róisín buys 50 candles for €4.20 each. She sells 35 for €9 each and the rest at 30% off. (a) What did the candles cost her? (b) What is the sale price of one candle? (c) Find her profit and her % profit on the cost price.
  3. The laptop. A laptop costs €738 including VAT at 23%. (a) Find the price before VAT. (b) How much VAT is paid? (c) Orla says "take 23% off €738". What answer does she get, and why is she wrong?
