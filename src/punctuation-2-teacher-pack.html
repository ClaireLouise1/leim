Junior Cycle English · Teacher pack

Punctuation 2: dialogue

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2015 specification): W11, W10

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can set out dialogue with the tag before or after the speech, put the right mark inside the closing speech mark, start a new line for each speaker, turn direct speech into reported speech, and choose a colon or a semicolon.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Right or Wrong? Sort (24 cut-up cards) · p.3 Dialogue Dictation A/B (speaking and listening) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Fix the Dialogue + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 2nd year. Time: about 60 minutes. All sentences are written by Léim.

Procedure

5 minLead-in. Write "I'm starving," said Dara. and Mam said, "Dinner is ready." on the board. Where is each comma? Why a comma, not a full stop, in the first? Build the four rules (free sheet, box 2).
12 minRight or Wrong? Sort (p.2, groups of 3, one set of 24 cards). Sort into right and wrong; for each wrong card, say the slip and write the fixed version on the back. 12 are right, 12 have one slip.
12 minDialogue Dictation (p.3, pairs). A reads four lines of dialogue aloud (words only); B writes them with every mark, a new line per speaker, then checks against A's card. Part 3: turn two lines into reported speech. Swap.
8 minColon or semicolon? Board: I packed three things ___ a torch, a map and tea. / Dara loves hurling ___ his sister prefers soccer. Thumbs up for colon, thumbs down for semicolon. Why can't a comma join two sentences?
18 minWorksheet (p.5). Fix the Dialogue in pairs, then the exam-style questions on "The Puffins" alone.
5 minExit ticket. Write two lines of dialogue: one with the tag after, one with the tag before. Homework: play Speech Marks Sprint at leim.ie/speech-marks-sprint.html.
Errors to look for: the comma or ? outside the closing speech mark · a full stop before said · Asked with a capital after ? · said inside the speech marks · no capital to start the speech · two speakers on one line · reported speech that keeps I / my or "is" · a comma joining two sentences · a colon straight after are or include. Support: the poster (p.4) on the desk. Fast finishers: write a split sentence: "I know," said Ava, "but it's late."

B. Exam-style questions: "The Puffins"

it's a shame said the ranger the puffins have gone Darragh asked are they coming back next April the ranger replied they always do
  1. Rewrite the passage above with correct punctuation, starting a new line for each new speaker.
  2. Change the ranger's first words into reported speech.
  3. Add a colon or a semicolon: (a) The boat trip had two highlights ___ the puffins and the seals. (b) The way out was calm ___ the way back was rough.
  4. Explain why The island was quiet, the birds had gone. is a run-on, and fix it two ways.
  5. Continue the scene with four more lines of dialogue between Darragh and the ranger.

Dialogue Dictation

Each partner's lines are on the other card. Reported: Liam said that he was hungry. · Liam asked if he could come to the party. · Orla said that her bike was broken. · Ciara asked if her dinner was ready.

A. Fix the Dialogue

"Hurry up" said Mam.No comma inside the closing mark: "Hurry up," said Mam.
"Where are you"? asked Niall.The question mark goes inside: "Where are you?" asked Niall.
"Watch out!" Shouted Dad.After ! the tag stays small: "Watch out!" shouted Dad.
Aoife said "it's my turn."Comma after said, capital to start the speech: Aoife said, "It's my turn."
The match was over, we went home.Two sentences joined by a comma (a run-on): The match was over; we went home. (or a full stop)
The kit includes: a jersey, shorts and socks.No colon straight after includes: The kit includes a jersey, shorts and socks. or The kit has three parts: a jersey, shorts and socks.

B. Exam-style questions

  1. "It's a shame," said the ranger. "The puffins have gone."
    Darragh asked, "Are they coming back?"
    "Next April," the ranger replied. "They always do."
  2. The ranger said that it was a shame and that the puffins had gone.
  3. (a) colon: two highlights: the puffins and the seals (b) semicolon: The way out was calm; the way back was rough.
  4. Two complete sentences joined by a comma. The island was quiet. The birds had gone. / The island was quiet; the birds had gone. (or …quiet because the birds had gone.)
  5. Reward correct speech marks with the mark inside, capitals, a new line per speaker, tags before and after, and dialogue that moves the scene on.

Marking tip

In the exam, punctuation is rewarded as part of the overall accuracy of a written answer rather than as a separate question. Our guide, not an official marking scheme.
