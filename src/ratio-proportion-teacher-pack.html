Junior Cycle Maths · Teacher pack

Ratio & proportion

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): N.2b, N.3a (currency, speed, distance and time come later under N.3b; bills, VAT and value-for-money money questions under N.2c)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can write and simplify ratios (including mixed units), share an amount in a ratio of two or three parts, work from one share or the difference, use the unitary method for recipes, prices and map scales, choose the best-value pack, and tell an absolute comparison from a relative one.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Share It Out mingle cards + Recipe Swap A/B cards · p.3 Ratio Dominoes (cut-up) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Error Hunt + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 1st year (Higher and Ordinary), or 3rd-year revision. Time: about 60 minutes. All questions are original.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: Who did better? Board "Class A: 12 of 20 passed. Class B: 15 of 30 passed." Hands up for A, B or the same. Ask both sides why. Don't settle it yet.
8 minGuided discovery: the sharing pattern. Pairs share €20, €45 and €100 in the ratio 2 : 3 any way they like (bar model on the board: 2 boxes and 3 boxes). What fraction does the first person always get? Why is it 2/5, not 2/3? Then €350 in 2 : 3 in their heads (€140, €210).
10 minShare It Out mingle (p.2, 24 cards = 12 question/answer pairs; one card each). Students with a question card work it out, then find the person holding the answer. Pairs check each other's working and sit together. Feedback: which pairs used "1 part"?
12 minRecipe Swap (p.2, pairs: Student A has the pancake recipe for 4 people, Student B for 6 people, each with gaps; don't show your card). Ask your partner for the amounts you're missing and work out your own column from theirs ("How much milk is in your recipe?"). Then both fill the 10-people column. Feedback: why does finding 1 person first always work?
10 minRatio Dominoes (p.3), groups of 3–4. Start with START, work out the clue, find the domino with that answer on its left, and build the chain to FINISH. Answer chain below.
12 minWorksheet (p.5). Error Hunt in pairs (find the mistake, name the rule, fix it), then the exam-style questions alone. Revisit the lead-in: Class A (60%) beat Class B (50%) relatively, B had more passes absolutely.
3 minExit ticket. Share €56 in the ratio 3 : 4; simplify 45 cm : 1.5 m (€24 and €32; 3 : 10). Homework: play Share It Out at leim.ie/share-it-out.html (Who did better? round) and copy three lines from the "Go back over these" list.

Errors to look for: dividing by 2 instead of the total number of parts · reversing the order (boys : girls v girls : boys) · writing 3 : 4 as 3/4 instead of 3/7 · adding to scale a recipe · not changing to the same units · cm → km in one step (÷ 100 000) · comparing 15 and 12 instead of 15/30 and 12/20. Support: draw the bar model every time. Fast finishers: share €100 in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3 to the nearest cent; which gets the leftover cent?

B. Exam-style questions (show all your work)

  1. Three friends share €480 in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. How much does each get?
  2. Concrete is made from cement, sand and gravel in the ratio 1 : 2 : 4. (a) How much sand is in 35 kg of concrete? (b) A builder has 12 kg of cement. How much gravel does she need?
  3. A map has a scale of 1 : 25 000. (a) A road is 14 cm long on the map. How long is it in km? (b) A lake is 1.5 km long. How long is it on the map?
  4. Juice comes in a 1.5 litre carton for €2.40 or a 500 ml carton for €0.85. Which is better value? Show your work.
  5. Team A won 18 of its 24 matches. Team B won 21 of its 30 matches. (a) Which team won more matches? (b) Which team had the better record? (c) Explain the difference.

A. Error Hunt

Simplify 12 : 16 = 6 : 8Not fully simplified. ÷ 4 (the HCF): 3 : 4
Share €35 in the ratio 2 : 5. Smaller share? = €17.50Split it in half. 2 + 5 = 7 parts, 1 part = €5: €10.00
Red : blue = 3 : 4. Fraction red? = 3/4The bottom must be the TOTAL parts (7): 3/7
Recipe for 4 uses 200 g. For 6? = 202 gAdded 2 instead of scaling. 50 g each × 6: 300 g
50 cm : 2 m in simplest form = 25 : 1Units not the same. 50 : 200: 1 : 4
A: 12 of 20 passed. B: 15 of 30 passed. Who did better? = B (15 > 12)Only absolute. Relatively A 60% v B 50%: A

B. Exam-style questions

  1. 12 parts, 1 part = €40: €120.00, €160.00, €200.00 (check: they add to €480)
  2. (a) 7 parts, 1 part = 5 kg, sand = 10 kg (b) gravel is 4 × the cement: 48 kg
  3. (a) 14 × 25 000 = 350 000 cm = 3.5 km (b) 1.5 km = 150 000 cm, ÷ 25 000 = 6 cm
  4. Per litre: €2.40 ÷ 1.5 = €1.60; €0.85 × 2 = €1.70. The 1.5 litre carton is better value.
  5. (a) Team B (21 v 18, absolute) (b) A 75% v B 70%: Team A (relative) (c) B played more matches, so it can win more of them while winning a smaller proportion.

Marking tip

Give credit for each step, as the exam does: "12 parts, 1 part = €40" earns marks even if a share slips. For "which is better?" insist on a reason (price of one, or the two percentages) and a clear conclusion.
