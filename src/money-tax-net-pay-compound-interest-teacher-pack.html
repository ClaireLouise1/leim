Money: tax, net pay & interest

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): N.2c, U.6 (bills, VAT and profit come first, in Money 1; currency in the speed, distance and time topic)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can work out gross pay (including overtime), income tax at the standard rate with a tax credit, net pay after deductions of given amounts, and compound interest on savings and loans for up to 3 years. Higher Level: the standard rate cut-off point and higher rate, deductions given as a %, and working backwards.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Payday mingle cards + Payslip Swap A/B cards · p.3 Money Dominoes (cut-up) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Error Hunt + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 2nd year (Higher and Ordinary), or 3rd-year revision. Time: about 60 minutes. All questions are original.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: the two savers. Board "€1,000 for 3 years at 10% a year. How much at the end?" Students answer alone, then compare. Collect answers (usually €1,300 and €1,331) without saying which is right.
8 minGuided discovery. Pairs fill a year-by-year table both ways: 10% of the first €1,000 each year, or 10% of what's in the account. Why does one pull ahead? What one number do we multiply by each year? (1.1.) Then: is a tax credit taken off the income or the tax? Try both on €40,000 with a €3,000 credit.
10 minPayday mingle (p.2, 24 cards = 12 question/answer pairs; one card each). Question card? Work it out and find the person with your answer. Answer card? Find your question. Pairs check each other's working.
12 minPayslip Swap (p.2, pairs, don't show your card). Each fact is on only ONE card. Ask your partner for what you're missing ("What's the overtime rate?"), then both fill every blank. Feedback: compare net pay (€594.37) and Emma's total (€6,618.78).
10 minMoney Dominoes (p.3), groups of 3–4. Start with START, work out the clue, find the domino with that answer on its left, and build the chain to FINISH.
12 minWorksheet (p.5). Error Hunt in pairs (find the mistake, name the rule, fix it), then the exam-style questions alone.
3 minExit ticket. €30,000 a year, credit €3,200, tax 20%: tax payable? €2,000 at 3% compound for 2 years: value? (€2,800; €2,121.80.) Homework: play Payslip Puzzle at leim.ie/payslip-puzzle.html and copy three lines from the "Go back over these" list.

Errors to look for: taking the tax credit off the income · adding the credit · paying overtime on all the hours · using 1.5 for 5% · simple interest instead of compound · giving the interest when the value is asked · taking the repayment off before adding the year's interest · going backwards by taking the % off. Support: give a blank year-by-year table (start · interest · end). Fast finishers: how many years at 10% until €1,000 doubles? (8 years: €2,143.59)

B. Exam-style questions (show all your work)

  1. Niamh earns €17.20 an hour for a basic 39-hour week and time-and-a-half for overtime. One week she works 45 hours. Find (a) her gross pay, (b) her tax if the rate is 20% and her weekly tax credit is €77, (c) her net pay if she also pays USC of €21.40 and PRSI of €33.02.
  2. €7,500 is invested for 3 years at 3% compound interest a year. Find the value at the end of the 3 years, to the nearest cent, and the interest earned.
  3. Pádraig earns €45,000 a year. His tax credit is €3,950 and the standard rate is 20%. He also pays USC of €1,180 and PRSI of €1,800. Find (a) his income tax, (b) his net income.
  4. Sorcha borrows €2,400 at 8% compound interest a year. She repays €1,000 at the end of year 1. How much does she owe at the end of year 2?
  5. Higher Level (a) Gross income €66,000, cut-off point €44,000, rates 20% and 40%, tax credits €4,000. Find the income tax. (b) €6,250 grows to €6,760 in 2 years at the same compound rate. Find the rate.

Income €40,000, tax credit €3,000, tax 20%. Tax payable? 20% of €37,000 = €7,400Took the credit off the income. €8,000 − €3,000 = €5,000.00
€5,000 at 4% compound for 2 years. Value? €5,000 + 8% = €5,400That's simple interest. €5,000 × 1.04² = €5,408.00
4 hours overtime at time-and-a-half; normal rate €12. 4 × €12 + 1.5 = €49.50Rate × 1.5 = €18 an hour. 4 × €18 = €72.00

Marking tip

Give credit for each step, as the exam does: gross pay, gross tax, tax payable and net pay each earn marks even if a later line slips; for compound interest, each year's value earns marks. For "explain" parts insist on a sentence with numbers in it.
