Linear equations

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): AF.2c, AF.4, U.3 (simultaneous equations, quadratic equations and inequalities come later, AF.4)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students understand "=" as balance; solve linear equations by doing the same to both sides, including letters on both sides, brackets and fractions; form an equation from words; and check a solution by substituting.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Equation Relay + Word-to-Equation Match cards · p.3 Equation Dominoes (cut-up) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Error Hunt + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 2nd year (Higher and Ordinary), or 3rd-year revision. Time: about 60 minutes. All questions are original.

Procedure

6 minLead-in: the scales. Draw a balance with 3 bags + 2 counters on the left and 14 counters on the right (as on the poster). "Each bag holds the same number. How many?" Take suggestions; ask what moves keep it level. Write the moves as algebra: 3x + 2 = 14 → 3x = 12 → x = 4.
8 minGuided discovery. Pairs solve 5x + 1 = 2x + 10 with the scales (take 2 bags off both sides first), then 3(x − 4) = 2(x + 1) and x/2 + x/3 = 10. What has to happen first each time? (Expand; clear the fractions by multiplying EVERY term by 6.) Check each answer by substituting.
10 minWord-to-Equation Match (p.2, 24 cards = 12 pairs; one card each). Word cards find their equation; pairs then solve it and check in the words. Feedback: "double it and add 5" v "add 5, then double it": why are the equations different?
10 minEquation Relay (p.2), teams of 4 in a line. Runner 1 solves and passes the answer (not the card) to runner 2, who uses it as p, and so on. The last runner brings the final number to you: if it's wrong, the team finds the broken step.
10 minEquation Dominoes (p.3), groups of 3–4: start with START, solve the clue, find the domino with that answer on its left and build the chain to FINISH.
13 minWorksheet (p.5). Error Hunt in pairs (find the mistake, name the rule, fix it), then the exam-style questions alone.
3 minExit ticket. Solve 4(x − 1) = 2x + 6; "I think of a number, multiply it by 3 and add 4. The answer is 25." (x = 5; 7). Homework: play Balance It at leim.ie/balance-it.html (From words and Check & fix rounds) and copy three lines from the "Go back over these" list.
Word-to-Equation Match key (word card ↔ equation card): 17 ↔ 8 (x = 6) · 5 ↔ 20 (x = 4) · 7 ↔ 18 (x = 7) · 11 ↔ 23 (x = 15) · 12 ↔ 9 (x = 16) · 4 ↔ 10 (x = 5) · 6 ↔ 1 (x = 50) · 19 ↔ 16 (x = 11) · 3 ↔ 2 (x = 2) · 21 ↔ 15 (x = 9) · 14 ↔ 22 (x = 14) · 24 ↔ 13 (x = 12).
Errors to look for: doing something to one side only · x + 8 = 3 → x = 11 · 4x = 20 → x = 16 · 3(x − 2) = 3x − 2 · moving a term without changing its sign · clearing fractions but not the whole number · −2x = 10 → x = 5. Support: draw the scales; write the move ("−2 both sides") beside each line; check by substituting. Fast finishers: make up a "think of a number" puzzle whose answer is −3 and swap with a partner.

A. Error Hunt

x + 8 = 3: x = 3 + 8 = 11Subtract 8 from both sides: x = −5
4x = 20: x = 20 − 4 = 164x means 4 × x, so divide by 4: x = 5

B. Exam-style questions

  1. 4x − 5 = 23 → 4x = 28 → x = 7 (check: 37 = 37)
  2. 6x + 3 = 4x + 13 → 2x = 10 → x = 5 (check: 33 = 33)
  3. × 12: 4(x + 1) + 3(x + 3) = 48 → 7x + 13 = 48 → 7x = 35 → x = 5 (check: 2 + 2 = 4)
  4. (a) 6x + 4(x + 4) = 116 (b) 10x + 16 = 116 → x = 10: 10 boxes of pencils, 14 boxes of pens (check: 60 + 56 = 116)
  5. (a) (3x + 10) + (x + 30) = 180 (b) 4x + 40 = 180 → x = 35: 115° and 65°

Marking tip

Give credit for each correct step, as the exam does: a correct equation from the words earns marks even if the solving slips, and a correct method carried through after an early slip still earns method marks. Expect students to check their answer by substituting.
