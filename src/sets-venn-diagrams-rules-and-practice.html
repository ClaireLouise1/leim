1. The language of sets

A set is a well-defined collection of elements. List it, A = {2, 4, 6, 8}, or give a rule: the even numbers from 1 to 9.

∈ / ∉is / is not an element of: 4 ∈ A, 5 ∉ A
#Athe number of elements: #A = 4
⊂subset (every element is also in A): {2, 8} ⊂ A
∅ or { }the empty (null) set
Uthe universal set: everything in the question (the box)

2. The four operations

U = {1, …, 8}, A = {1, 2, 3, 4, 6}, B = {2, 4, 6, 8}

A ∩ Bin A and B{2, 4, 6}
A ∪ Bin A or B (or both){1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8}
A \ Bin A but not B{1, 3}
A′not in A{5, 7, 8}
Order: A ∩ B = B ∩ A and A ∪ B = B ∪ A (commutative); (A ∩ B) ∩ C = A ∩ (B ∩ C), same for ∪ (associative). But A \ B ≠ B \ A.

4. Counting

#(A ∪ B) = #A + #B − #(A ∩ B): adding #A and #B counts the overlap twice.

5. Survey questions

Fill the middle first (call it x if unknown). Then each circle "only" = total − overlap (15 − x). "Neither" goes outside the circles, inside the box. All regions add up to the total: solve for x.

3 sets: centre → the three "two only" parts → the "one only" parts → outside. "Exactly two" leaves out the centre; "at least two" includes it.

Exam tip: draw the box for U and label every circle. List each element once, in set brackets { }. An empty answer is ∅, not 0. Read "only", "neither", "exactly" and "at least" carefully, and show the equation when you find x.

E. Exam-style questions (show your work)

  1. 30 students were asked if they play rugby (R) or soccer (S). 17 play rugby, 14 play soccer and 4 play neither. x play both. (a) Draw a Venn diagram. (b) Find x. (c) How many play rugby only?
  2. 120 people were asked how they read the news. 70 read it online, 45 read a paper and 20 do both. (a) How many do neither? (b) How many use exactly one?
  3. 40 students were asked which languages they study: French (F), German (G), Spanish (S). 2 study all three; 5 study French and German, 6 German and Spanish, 7 French and Spanish; 18 study French, 15 German and 20 Spanish. (a) Draw the Venn diagram. (b) How many study none? (c) How many study exactly two?
