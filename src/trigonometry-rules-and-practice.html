Label the sides from the angle θ
Elevation: looking UP from the horizontal
Depression: looking DOWN from the horizontal

1. Name the sides

Hypotenuse: opposite the right angle (longest). Opposite: across from θ. Adjacent: next to θ. Change the angle and opposite/adjacent swap.

2. SOH CAH TOA

SOHsin θ = O ÷ H
CAHcos θ = A ÷ H
TOAtan θ = O ÷ A

Use the ratio with the side you have and the side you want. The ratio depends only on the angle, not on the size of the triangle (similar triangles).

3. Finding a side

x on top: ×
sin 35° = x ÷ 12
x = 12 × sin 35° = 6.9
x on the bottom: ÷
sin 40° = 8 ÷ x
x = 8 ÷ sin 40° = 12.4
An angle: ⁻¹
tan θ = 5 ÷ 8
θ = tan⁻¹(5 ÷ 8) = 32°

4. The calculator

Always in DEG mode: sin 30° must give 0.5 (−0.988 means RAD). sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹ are on SHIFT. Keep the full value until the last step.

5. Elevation and depression

Both are measured from the horizontal. The angle of depression from the top equals the angle of elevation from the bottom (alternate angles). Measured from someone's eyes? Add their eye height at the end.

Higher Level: two angles from one point: find both heights, subtract. One ratio from another: tan A = 3/4 → sides 3, 4, 5 (Pythagoras) → sin A = 3/5.

Classic mistakes: RAD mode · labelling from the wrong angle · × when x is on the bottom · sin instead of sin⁻¹ · depression measured from the vertical cliff · rounding too early.
Exam tip: write the ratio with the numbers in it (tan 32° = h ÷ 18) before you press any buttons, then round only at the end and give units.

E. Exam-style questions (show your work)

  1. A ramp. A ramp is 6 m long and makes an angle of 4° with the ground. Find (a) the height it rises and (b) the horizontal distance it covers, both to 2 decimal places.
  2. The clinometer. Oisín stands 30 m from a building. The angle of elevation of the top is 41° and his eyes are 1.6 m above the ground. (a) Find the height of the building. (b) What is the angle of elevation from 50 m away?
  3. A cliff. From the top of an 80 m cliff, the angle of depression of a boat is 12°. (a) How far is the boat from the foot of the cliff? (b) How far is it from the top of the cliff in a straight line?
  4. Sides and angles. Triangle PQR has |∠PQR| = 90°, |PQ| = 9 cm and |PR| = 15 cm. (a) Find |QR|. (b) Write sin ∠QPR as a fraction. (c) Find |∠QPR| to the nearest degree.
