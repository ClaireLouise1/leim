Probability 2: relative frequency & counting

1. Relative frequency

Relative frequency = times it happened ÷ number of trials

A drawing pin is dropped 50 times and lands point up 18 times: 18 ÷ 50 = 0.36 (= 36% = 9/25). Divide by the number of trials, not by "the rest".

2. Estimating a probability

When outcomes are not equally likely (a pin, a bent coin, a penalty), do an experiment: the relative frequency is your estimate of the probability. The more trials, the better the estimate.

To combine results, add the counts first: 12 of 30 and 18 of 50 → 30 of 80 = 0.375.

3. Expected frequency

Expected frequency = probability × number of trials

Fair die, 300 rolls: expect 1/6 × 300 = 50 sixes (about 50, not exactly). Pin from section 1, 1,000 drops: 0.36 × 1,000 = 360.

4. Fair or biased?

Compare the results with what you'd expect. A die rolled 600 times: expect about 100 of each.

Die A: all close to 100, so fair. Die B: a 6 came up about twice as often as expected, so probably biased. Only a few trials? You can't tell.

5. The fundamental principle of counting

m ways, then n ways → m × n ways together

3 starters, 4 mains, 2 desserts: 3 × 4 × 2 = 24 meals. A lock with 3 dials (0–9): 10 × 10 × 10 = 1,000 codes.

6. When you can't repeat

The choices go down: 4 friends in a row: 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 24. Gold, silver and bronze from 8 runners: 8 × 7 × 6 = 336. A 3-dial code set at random: P(7 1 3) = 1/1,000.

Exam tip: show the division ("18 ÷ 50 = 0.36"), write the answer the way the question asks (decimal, % or fraction), give reasons with numbers ("a 6 came up 205 times but we'd expect about 100"), and multiply, don't add, when counting.

A. Relative frequency (give decimals unless asked otherwise)

  1. A drawing pin is dropped 50 times and lands point up 18 times. Find the relative frequency of point up.
  2. Aoife takes 40 penalties and scores 34. Find the relative frequency of scoring.
  3. Cian flips a bottle 80 times and it lands standing up 12 times. Find the relative frequency as a percentage.
  4. Of 250 seeds planted, 215 grew. Find the relative frequency of a seed growing.
  5. A coin is tossed 20 times and lands heads 9 times. Find the relative frequency of heads as a fraction.
  6. In a survey of 200 cars, 46 were red. Estimate the probability that the next car is red.
  7. A die is rolled 120 times and a 6 comes up 26 times. Relative frequency of a 6 as a fraction?
  8. A cup is thrown 25 times and lands on its side 17 times. Find the relative frequency of landing on its side.
