Junior Cycle Spanish · Teacher pack

Holidays & travel

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 1.11, 1.19, 1.20, 3.1 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 1.10, 1.15, 3.1 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can talk about a holiday in three time frames (normalmente voy · el verano pasado fui · el verano que viene voy a ir), say how they travelled (en avión, a pie), where they stayed, what the weather was like and what they did, ask tú questions in the past (¿Adónde fuiste? ¿Qué hiciste?), and write a holiday postcard.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Holiday Swap info gap (two cards) · p.3–4 Holiday Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 2nd–3rd year. Time: about 60 minutes.

Procedure

5 minLead-in. Show (or describe) a holiday photo of "your" summer. Students guess: ¿Fuiste a España? ¿Fuiste en avión? Answer only sí / no until they find where, how and with whom. Write the winning questions up.
10 minGuided discovery. Board: Normalmente voy a Kerry. / El verano pasado fui a Francia. / El verano que viene voy a ir a Italia. Ask: "Which is a habit, which is finished, which is a plan? Which words told you?" Students sort time phrases (ayer, hace dos años, todos los años, mañana…) into three columns. Concept checks: "Ayer… voy or fui?" "Viajé en avión or a avión?" "Which transport takes a, not en?" (a pie).
5 minPoster (p.5). Drill the three tenses with ir, viajar, alojarse and the weather, then the postcard checklist.
15 minHoliday Swap (p.2), pairs A and B. A is Lucía, B is Mateo. Each asks the eight tú questions and writes the partner's answers. Step 2: they ask about a real or invented holiday. Fast pairs report back in the third person: Mateo fue a Galicia con sus amigos…
10 minHoliday Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Deal out the dominoes; players match a Spanish phrase to its English. First to place all their dominoes wins. Answer chain below.
10 minWorksheet (p.6) A and B in pairs. Feed back on the error correction.
5 minExit ticket. Three sentences, three tenses: where you usually go, where you went last summer, where you're going to go next year. Homework: play Postcard Builder at leim.ie/postcard-builder.html and write one postcard from the game in your copy.
Errors to listen for: Ayer voy a la playa (fui) · Viajé a avión (en avión; only a pie) · Voy visitar (voy a visitar) · sacé (saqué) · hació (hizo) · Fui en Francia (a Francia; estoy en Francia) · Querido Ana (Querida) · ¿Dónde fuiste? (¿Adónde…? is best with ir).
Culture note: in Spain most families take their holidays in August, when many city businesses close; many go to the coast or back to el pueblo, the family's home village. A puente is a long weekend made by taking the day between a holiday and the weekend. The Camino de Santiago ends in Galicia: walkers need at least the last 100 km for the Compostela certificate. Support: give weaker students the poster as a mat. Fast finishers: write Lucía's or Mateo's postcard from their answers.

Domino answer chain:

  1. I sunbathed on the beach. = Tomé el sol en la playa.
  2. I swam in the sea. = Me bañé en el mar.
  3. I took lots of photos. = Saqué muchas fotos.
  4. I went to France. = Fui a Francia.
  5. The journey lasted two hours. = El viaje duró dos horas.
  6. I went sightseeing. = Hice turismo.
  7. It was very hot. = Hizo mucho calor.
  8. We travelled by plane. = Viajamos en avión.
  9. I tried the local food. = Probé la comida típica.
  10. last year = el año pasado
  11. Usually we go to the coast. = Normalmente vamos a la costa.
  12. I'm going to go to Italy. = Voy a ir a Italia.
  13. next summer = el verano que viene
  14. I went on foot. = Fui a pie.
  15. I stayed at a campsite. = Me alojé en un camping.
  16. I had a great time. = Lo pasé bomba.
  17. I'm having a great time! = ¡Lo estoy pasando bomba!
  18. I'd like to go to Greece. = Me gustaría ir a Grecia.
  19. a youth hostel = un albergue juvenil
  20. I bought souvenirs. = Compré recuerdos.

Worksheet

Irene's summer in Mallorca

↗ Léim

A. Read Irene's email

¡Hola! ¿Qué tal tus vacaciones? Yo estoy en Mallorca, en las islas Baleares, con mis tíos y mis primos. Vinimos en barco desde Barcelona: el viaje duró siete horas, pero dormimos en un camarote, ¡qué guay! Nos alojamos en una casa rural cerca de un pueblo pequeño, en la montaña. No hay piscina, pero la playa está a veinte minutos en coche. Los primeros días hizo muchísimo calor, unos treinta y cinco grados. Ayer fuimos a Palma, la capital: visitamos la catedral, hicimos turismo por el casco antiguo y compré recuerdos para mis amigos. Por la noche probé la ensaimada, un dulce típico de Mallorca. ¡Riquísima! Mañana vamos a hacer senderismo en la sierra de Tramuntana, y el domingo vamos a volver a Madrid en avión. El verano que viene me gustaría ir a Irlanda para aprender inglés. ¿Y tú? ¿Qué hiciste este verano? Un abrazo, Irene
  1. Where is Irene, and who is she with?
  2. How did they travel there, and how long did it take?
  3. Where did they sleep on the journey?
  4. Where are they staying, and where is it?
  5. What doesn't it have, and how far is the beach?
  6. What was the weather like at first?
  7. What three things did they do in Palma?
  8. What did she try last night?
  9. What are they going to do tomorrow and on Sunday?
  10. Where would she like to go next summer, and why?

B. Find and fix the mistake

  1. Ayer voy a la playa con mis primos.
  2. El año pasado viajamos a tren.
  3. Mañana voy visitar el museo.
  4. Ayer sacé muchas fotos.
  1. Ayer hace mucho sol.
  2. Me alojé en un hotel muy bonita.
  3. El verano que viene fui a Grecia.
  4. Querido Ana: ¡Hola desde Roma!

C. Now you

Write a reply to Irene (50–70 words). Answer her question: where you went this summer (or last summer), how you travelled, where you stayed, the weather, what you did, and where you're going to go next year.

Answers. A: 1 In Mallorca (the Balearic Islands), with her aunt and uncle and her cousins. 2 By boat from Barcelona; seven hours. 3 In a cabin on the boat. 4 In a country house near a small village, in the mountains. 5 A swimming pool; 20 minutes by car. 6 Very, very hot, about 35 degrees. 7 Visited the cathedral, went sightseeing in the old town, bought souvenirs. 8 An ensaimada, a typical Mallorcan pastry. 9 Tomorrow: go hiking in the Tramuntana mountains. Sunday: fly back to Madrid. 10 Ireland, to learn English. · B: 11 fui 12 en tren 13 voy a visitar 14 saqué 15 hizo 16 bonito 17 voy a ir 18 Querida.
Watch out: vinimos = we came (venir); el camarote = cabin; el casco antiguo = the old town; la ensaimada = a spiral pastry from Mallorca; la sierra = mountain range.
