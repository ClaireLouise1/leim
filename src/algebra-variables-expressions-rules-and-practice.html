1. The words

A variable is a letter standing for a number that can change. An expression is made of terms joined by + and − signs: 4x² − 3x + 7 has 3 terms. The coefficient of x is the number in front, sign included: −3. The constant is the term with no letter: 7.

3. Collecting like terms

Like terms have exactly the same letters and powers (3x and −5x; xy and yx). 3x and 3x² are not like terms. Each term keeps the sign in front of it.

5a + 3b − 2a + b = 3a + 4b
2x² + 5x − x² − 8x + 3 = x² − 3x + 3
x² + x² = 2x² (not x⁴) · 3x + 2y can't be simplified

4. Words into algebra

"5 more than n" → n + 5 · "5 less than n" → n − 5 (not 5 − n) · "double n, then subtract 3" → 2n − 3 · "half of n" → n/2.

Tickets at €8 each plus a €3 booking fee: n tickets cost 8n + 3 (the fee is paid once). Perimeter: add all the sides.

5. Substitution

Put each number in brackets, then follow BIMDAS.

FindWorking
5 − 2x, x = −35 − 2(−3) = 5 + 6 = 11
x², x = −4(−4)² = 16 (not −16)
3x², x = −23 × 4 = 12 (square first)
ab − b, a = −2, b = 5−10 − 5 = −15

2x when x = 3 is 2 × 3 = 6, not 23. A number next to a letter means multiply.

Exam tip: use brackets for negative numbers and write the line where you put them in: it earns marks even if the answer slips. Check an expression by trying a number: 2 tickets should cost 8(2) + 3 = €19.

E. Exam-style questions (show your work)

  1. Simplify (a) 6x − 2y + 3x + 5y (b) 3n² + 2n − n² − 7n + 4
  2. p = −3 and q = 2. Find the value of (a) 2p + 5q (b) p² − q (c) pq − q²
  3. A gym charges a €30 joining fee plus €12 a month. (a) Write an expression for the cost, in euro, of m months. (b) Find the cost of one year. (c) Another gym charges €15 a month with no joining fee. Which gym is cheaper for one year?
  4. A triangle has sides x + 4, 2x − 1 and 3x cm. (a) Write the perimeter in its simplest form. (b) Find the perimeter when x = 5. (c) How long is the longest side when x = 5?
