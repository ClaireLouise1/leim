Expanding & simplifying

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): AF.3a, AF.3b. Play the game: leim.ie/bracket-blaster.html

1. Multiplying terms

Numbers × numbers, letters × letters: 3x × 4x = 12x² · 2a × 5b = 10ab · x² × x = x³ (add the powers). Same signs give +, different signs give −.

2. One bracket

Multiply every term inside:

3(x − 4)3x − 12 (not 3x − 4)
−2(x − 5)−2x + 10 (− × − = +)
x(x + 3)x² + 3x
−(x − 6)−x + 6 (every sign changes)

3. Expand, then simplify

3(x + 2) − 2(x − 4)
= 3x + 6 − 2x + 8
= x + 14 (collect like terms last)

4. Squares

(x + 4)² = (x + 4)(x + 4) = x² + 8x + 16
(x − 3)² = x² − 6x + 9 (not x² − 9 or x² + 9)
(2x + 5)² = 4x² + 20x + 25

5. Double brackets: the area model

Every term × every term. One bracket along the top, one down the side; each box = row × column; add the boxes.

(x + 3)(x − 5)
= x² − 5x + 3x − 15
= x² − 2x − 15

Spot the pattern: (x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a + b)x + ab. The middle number is the two numbers added, the last is the two multiplied: (x + 6)(x − 2) = x² + 4x − 12.

(2x + 1)(x − 4) = 2x² − 8x + x − 4 = 2x² − 7x − 4
(a + 2)(b − 3) = ab − 3a + 2b − 6 (no like terms)

6. Difference of two squares

(x + 7)(x − 7) = x² − 7x + 7x − 49 = x² − 49: the middle terms cancel.

7. Higher Level: three terms

(x + 2)(x² − x + 3) = x³ − x² + 3x + 2x² − 2x + 6 = x³ + x² + x + 6

Exam tip: write all four terms of a double bracket before you collect, and write (x + 3)² as (x + 3)(x + 3) first. Check with a number: put x = 1 into the question and your answer: they must give the same value.
