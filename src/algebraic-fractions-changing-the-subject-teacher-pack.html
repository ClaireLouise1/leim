Algebraic fractions & changing the subject

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): AF.3a, AF.3c, AF.6

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can add and subtract algebraic fractions with number denominators, divide a quadratic by a linear expression (long division), and change the subject of a formula, then use the new formula. Higher Level: fractions with x in the denominator, cubic ÷ linear, formulas with squares and square roots, and the subject appearing twice.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Rearrange Match mingle cards + Formula Swap A/B cards · p.3 Rearranging Dominoes (cut-up) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Error Hunt + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 3rd year (Higher and Ordinary). Time: about 60 minutes. All questions are original.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: think of a number. "I thought of a number, multiplied by 3 and added 5. I got 26. What was it?" Students undo it: − 5, ÷ 3. Write y = 3x + 5 and the reverse flow y → − 5 → ÷ 3 → x on the board: that's changing the subject.
8 minGuided discovery. Pairs do ½ + ⅓, then x/2 + x/3, then (x + 1)/2 + (x + 4)/3 the same way. Elicit: same bottom first, every top in a bracket. Then 156 ÷ 12 by long division next to (x² + 5x + 6) ÷ (x + 2): same four steps.
10 minRearrange Match mingle (p.2, 24 cards = 12 formula cards + 12 answer cards; one card each). Formula card? Rearrange it, then find the card with your answer. Answer card? Find your formula. Pairs check by putting in easy numbers.
12 minFormula Swap (p.2, pairs, don't show your card). Each number is on only ONE card. Ask your partner for what you're missing, rearrange the formula together, then put the numbers in. Feedback: phone bill → 160 minutes; garden → 11 m.
10 minRearranging Dominoes (p.3), groups of 3–4. Start with START, work out the clue, find the domino with that answer on its left, and build the chain to FINISH.
12 minWorksheet (p.5). Error Hunt in pairs (find the mistake, name it, fix it), then the exam-style questions alone. Question 5 is Higher Level.
3 minExit ticket. Make x the subject of y = 2x − 7, then write x/4 + x/6 as one fraction. (x = (y + 7)/2; 5x/12.) Homework: play Rearrange Race at leim.ie/rearrange-race.html.
Mingle key (formula card → answer card): 7 → 13 · 16 → 18 · 3 → 6 · 1 → 9 · 22 → 14 · 10 → 24 · 5 → 8 · 4 → 12 · 17 → 11 · 19 → 20 · 23 → 21 · 15 → 2.
Errors to look for: adding the bottoms · not changing both signs after a minus · moving a term before multiplying away a fraction · dividing only one term · subtracting wrongly in long division · π outside the square root. Support: "What was done to x first? Undo that last." Fast finishers: make up a formula whose rearranged form is x = (y + 4)/5 in two different ways.

B. Exam-style questions (show all your work)

  1. Write (2x − 1)/3 + (x + 4)/2 as a single fraction.
  2. Divide 3x² + 11x − 4 by x + 4. Check your answer by multiplying.
  3. Hiring a bike costs C = 6 + 4h euro for h hours. (a) Make h the subject. (b) Sinéad paid €30. For how many hours did she hire the bike?

A. Error Hunt

x/2 + x/3Added the bottoms. Common bottom 6: 5x/6
(x + 3)/2 − (x − 1)/4 = (2x + 6 − x − 1)/4−(x − 1) = −x + 1: (x + 7)/4

y = (x − 2)/5. Make x the subject.Multiply by 5, don't divide: 5y = x − 2, x = 5y + 2
(x² + 7x + 10) ÷ (x + 2). Line 1: x² ÷ x = xAfter x(x + 2), subtract: 7x − 2x = 5x, so x + 5
Higher Level: A = πr². Make r the subject.π must be inside the root: r = √(A/π)

B. Exam-style questions

  1. (2(2x − 1) + 3(x + 4))/6 = (4x − 2 + 3x + 12)/6 = (7x + 10)/6
  2. 3x² ÷ x = 3x; 3x(x + 4) = 3x² + 12x; subtract: −x − 4; −x ÷ x = −1. Answer 3x − 1. Check: (3x − 1)(x + 4) = 3x² + 11x − 4 ✓
  3. (a) C − 6 = 4h → h = (C − 6)/4 (b) (30 − 6)/4 = 6 hours
  4. (a) v² − u² = 2as → s = (v² − u²)/(2a) (b) (400 − 100)/10 = 30
  5. (a) (4(x + 2) − (x − 3))/((x − 3)(x + 2)) = (3x + 11)/((x − 3)(x + 2)) (b) xy − 3y = 2x + 1 → xy − 2x = 3y + 1 → x(y − 2) = 3y + 1 → x = (3y + 1)/(y − 2)

Marking tip

Give credit for each step, as the exam does: the common denominator, the expanded top, the simplified answer. In long division look for like terms lined up and a check by multiplying. When rearranging, one operation per line; in "use the formula" parts accept a correct substitution into a correctly rearranged formula.
