Junior Cycle Spanish · Teacher pack

Ser, estar & hay

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 2.1, 2.5 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 2.1, 2.4 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can choose between ser (who, what it's like, job, origin, time), estar (location, feelings, condition, action now) and hay (there is / there are), use all six forms of ser and estar, tell hay un… from el … está, and recognise adjectives that change meaning (es aburrido / está aburrido).

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Party Detectives info gap (two cards) · p.3–4 Ser-Estar Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 1st–3rd year. Time: about 60 minutes.

Procedure

5 minLead-in. Introduce a "mystery person" (a photo or a stick figure): Es Carmen. Es de Málaga. Es médica. Hoy está muy cansada. Está en el hospital. Students guess in English what each sentence tells them. Write the sentences in two columns without labels.
10 minGuided discovery. "Why are these sentences in two groups? What does the left column tell you about Carmen? And the right?" Elicit who / what she's like (ser) vs where / how she is now (estar). Add Hay un hospital en mi pueblo.: "What does hay do?" Then Hay un banco / El banco está…: "What comes after hay? After está?" Concept checks: "Estoy de Irlanda: right or wrong?" "Es aburrido or está aburrido: which one is an insult?"
5 minPoster (p.5). Drill the six forms of both verbs as a chant, then DOCTOR / PLACE and the rhyme "How you feel and where you are…".
15 minParty Detectives (p.2), pairs A and B. Each card has half the facts about six guests. Students ask ¿De dónde es…? ¿Cómo es…? ¿Cómo está…? ¿Dónde está…? and fill the gaps, then write three sentences that use both verbs (Hugo es serio, pero hoy está aburrido). Check: Pablo is in the kitchen, Lucía is nervous, Sara is from Cork.
10 minSer-Estar Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Players match a Spanish phrase to its English. Watch the pairs that change meaning (es lista / está lista). Answer chain below.
10 minWorksheet (p.6) A and B in pairs. Feed back on the error correction: students say why (DOCTOR or PLACE?).
5 minExit ticket. Three sentences about your town: one with es, one with está, one with hay. Homework: play Verb Swipe at leim.ie/verb-swipe.html and copy five sentences from the "Go back over these" list.
Errors to listen for: Soy cansado (estoy) · Estoy de Irlanda / Estoy estudiante (soy) · Madrid es en el centro (está) · Hay el supermercado (el supermercado está) · Está un parque (hay un parque) · Es las tres (son) · esta without the accent · Mis padres es… (son / están).
Language awareness: Irish students already know two "to be" verbs: Is múinteoir mé (identity, like ser) and Tá mé tuirseach (state, like estar). Point this out: it often makes the rule click. Support: give weaker students the poster as a mat. Fast finishers: describe a famous person with three ser and three estar sentences (where they are today, how they feel) for the class to guess.

Worksheet

Marta's email from Toledo

↗ Léim

A. Read Marta's email

¡Hola, Ciarán! ¿Qué tal estás? Yo estoy muy contenta porque hoy es viernes y mañana es la fiesta de cumpleaños de mi hermano Pablo. Te cuento un poco de mi ciudad. Vivo en Toledo, que está en el centro de España, muy cerca de Madrid. Toledo es una ciudad antigua y muy bonita: hay una catedral enorme, un castillo (el Alcázar) y muchas calles estrechas. Mi casa está en el casco antiguo, al lado de una plaza. En mi calle hay una panadería y dos cafeterías, pero no hay supermercado. Mi familia es pequeña: mi madre es médica y mi padre es cocinero en un restaurante. Mi hermano Pablo es muy gracioso, pero hoy está nervioso porque la fiesta es en casa y ¡su habitación está muy desordenada! Ahora son las seis de la tarde y estoy en mi dormitorio. Estoy escribiendo este correo y mi gato, Rayo, está encima de mi cama. ¿Y tú? ¿Cómo es tu pueblo? ¿Qué hay en tu calle? Un abrazo, Marta
  1. How does Marta feel, and why?
  2. Where is Toledo?
  3. What is Toledo like?
  4. Name three things there are in Toledo.
  5. Where exactly is her house?
  6. What is there in her street, and what isn't there?
  7. What do her parents do?
  8. What is Pablo like, and how is he today? Why?
  9. What time is it, and where is Marta?
  10. What is she doing, and where is Rayo?

B. Find and fix the mistake

  1. Mi madre está profesora.
  2. Soy cansado hoy.
  3. Madrid es en el centro de España.
  4. Está un parque en mi pueblo.
  1. Hay el baño al lado de la cocina.
  2. Mis amigos es muy simpáticos.
  3. Estoy de Irlanda.
  4. Es las cuatro de la tarde.

C. Now you

Write Ciarán's reply to Marta (50–70 words). Answer her two questions, say what your family members do, and say how you feel today and where you are right now.

Answers. A: 1 Very happy: it's Friday and tomorrow is her brother Pablo's birthday party. 2 In the centre of Spain, very near Madrid. 3 Old and very pretty. 4 A huge cathedral, a castle (the Alcázar), lots of narrow streets. 5 In the old town, next to a square. 6 A bakery and two cafés; no supermarket. 7 Her mum is a doctor; her dad is a cook in a restaurant. 8 Very funny; nervous today because the party is at home and his room is very messy. 9 6 pm; in her bedroom. 10 Writing this email; on her bed. · B: 11 es profesora 12 Estoy cansado 13 está en el centro 14 Hay un parque 15 El baño está al lado 16 son 17 Soy de Irlanda 18 Son las cuatro.
Watch out: el casco antiguo = the old town; estrechas = narrow; la panadería = the bakery; desordenada = messy; la fiesta es en casa (an event: ser).
