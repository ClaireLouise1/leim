Junior Cycle Maths · Teacher pack

Trigonometry

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes (2018 specification): GT.4, U.6

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can label the opposite, adjacent and hypotenuse from a given angle, use sin, cos and tan (SOH CAH TOA) to find a side, use sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ and tan⁻¹ to find an angle (0°–90°), and solve real-life problems with angles of elevation and depression.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Clinometer mingle cards + Height Swap A/B · p.3 Trigonometry Dominoes (cut-up) · p.4 Wall poster · p.5 Worksheet: Error Hunt + exam-style questions · p.6 Answers. Level: 3rd year (Higher and Ordinary). Time: about 60 minutes. All questions are original.

Procedure

8 minLead-in: same angle, same ratio. Each pair draws a right-angled triangle with a 35° angle, any size. Measure the opposite and the hypotenuse and work out opposite ÷ hypotenuse. Collect answers on the board: all about 0.57. Then everyone types sin 35°. Name the three sides and build SOH CAH TOA.
5 minDEG check. Everyone types sin 30°. Anyone who doesn't get 0.5 switches to DEG mode now.
10 minClinometer mingle (p.2, 24 cards = 12 question/answer pairs; one card each, calculators allowed). Question card? Work it out and find your answer. Answer card? Find your question. Pairs say which ratio they used.
12 minHeight Swap (p.2, pairs, one A and one B card). Don't show your card. Each fact is on only one card: ask your partner for what you're missing, sketch each triangle, label O, A, H and fill every blank.
12 minTrigonometry Dominoes (p.3), groups of 3–4, calculators allowed. Start with START, work out the clue, find the domino with that answer on its left, and build the chain to FINISH. Sides are to 1 d.p., angles to the nearest degree.
10 minWorksheet (p.5). Error Hunt in pairs, then the exam-style questions alone. Optional: make a clinometer from a protractor, a straw and a weight on a string and measure the height of the school.
3 minExit ticket. A 10 m ladder makes 70° with the ground. How high up the wall does it reach? Homework: play Clinometer Challenge at leim.ie/clinometer-challenge.html.

B. Exam-style questions (show all your work)

  1. A kite string is 75 m long and makes an angle of 48° with the ground. (a) How high is the kite, to 1 d.p.? (b) The wind drops and the angle becomes 35°. How much lower is the kite?
  2. A ramp must be no steeper than 6°. It has to rise 0.5 m. Find the shortest possible length of the ramp, to 2 d.p.
  3. Róisín stands 22 m from a tree. With a clinometer she measures the angle of elevation of the top as 37°. Her eyes are 1.5 m above the ground. Find the height of the tree, to 1 d.p.
  4. Higher Level. From the top of a 55 m lighthouse, the angles of depression of two boats in a line with it are 28° and 16°. How far apart are the boats, to 1 d.p.?
  5. In triangle ABC, |∠ABC| = 90°, |AC| = 20 cm and |∠BAC| = 36°. Find |BC| and |AB| to 1 d.p., and the area of the triangle.
For the teacher

Answers

↗ Léim

Height Swap

Tree: 20 × tan 35° = 14.0 m · Kite: 40 × sin 50° = 30.6 m · Ramp: sin⁻¹(0.6 ÷ 5) = 7° · Boat: the angle at the boat is 20° (alternate angles), 50 ÷ tan 20° = 137.4 m · Ladder: cos⁻¹(1.5 ÷ 6) = 76°.

A. Error Hunt

Hypotenuse 10 cm, angle 40°: find the opposite side. 10 × cos 40° = 7.7 cmThe opposite needs SOH: 10 × sin 40° = 6.4 cm
Opposite 6 m, angle 35°: find the hypotenuse. 6 × sin 35° = 3.4 mx is on the bottom, so DIVIDE: 6 ÷ sin 35° = 10.5 m
Opposite 5 cm, adjacent 9 cm: find θ. tan(5 ÷ 9) = 0.0097Use tan⁻¹ (SHIFT tan): tan⁻¹(5 ÷ 9) = 29°
Find sin 30°. sin 30 = −0.988Calculator in RAD mode. In DEG: 0.5
A 40 m cliff, angle of depression 25° to a boat. How far is the boat from the cliff? 40 × tan 25° = 18.7 mThe angle at the boat is 25°, and the cliff is opposite it: 40 ÷ tan 25° = 85.8 m
A 5 m ladder, foot 1.2 m from the wall. Angle with the ground? sin⁻¹(1.2 ÷ 5) = 14°1.2 m is ADJACENT to the angle at the foot: cos⁻¹(1.2 ÷ 5) = 76°

B. Exam-style questions

  1. (a) 75 × sin 48° = 55.7 m. (b) 75 × sin 35° = 43.0… m, so it is 55.73… − 43.01… = 12.7 m lower.
  2. sin 6° = 0.5 ÷ L, so L = 0.5 ÷ sin 6° = 4.78 m (any shorter ramp would be steeper than 6°).
  3. 22 × tan 37° = 16.57… m. Add the eye height: 16.57… + 1.5 = 18.1 m.
  4. The angles at the boats are 28° and 16°. Near boat: 55 ÷ tan 28° = 103.4… m. Far boat: 55 ÷ tan 16° = 191.8… m. Apart: 88.4 m.
  5. |BC| = 20 × sin 36° = 11.8 cm · |AB| = 20 × cos 36° = 16.2 cm · Area = ½ × 16.18… × 11.75… = 95.1 cm².

Marking tip

Give credit for each step, as the exam does: the sides labelled from the angle, the right ratio written with the numbers in it, the calculator step, then rounding and units. An answer from a calculator in RAD mode loses the calculation marks but can keep the method marks.
