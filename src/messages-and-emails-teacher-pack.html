Junior Cycle Spanish · Teacher pack

Messages, emails & forms

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 1.16, 1.17, 1.21, 1.22 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 1.13, 2.8 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can open and close an email to a friend and a formal email correctly (Querida Ana: … Un abrazo / Estimado señor: … Atentamente), keep to tú or usted, use set phrases (Gracias por tu correo, Le escribo para…, Escríbeme pronto), answer the questions in a message, understand and fill in a Spanish form, say an email address aloud, and check their own writing for the most common slips.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Sign-up Desk info gap (two cards) · p.3–4 Email Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 2nd–3rd year. Time: about 60 minutes.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: Who's it to? Write four lines on the board: Querida abuela: · Estimado señor: · Un abrazo, Seán · nos vemos a las 6 bss. Pairs guess who each one is written to (a granny? a hotel? a friend? a chat?) and how they know.
10 minGuided discovery. Put the two short emails from the poster (p.5) side by side. "Find every word that means you or your. What's different?" (te, tu, haces vs le, su, tiene). "What comes after the name?" (a colon). "Why Querida and not Querido?" Concept checks: "Writing to a campsite: tu or su?" "Un abrazo to a hotel: OK?"
5 minPoster (p.5). Drill the starts and ends; then dictate two email addresses (arroba, punto, guion bajo) for students to write.
15 minSign-up Desk info gap (p.2), pairs A and B. Students are signing up for a summer camp in Spain. Step 1: ask the questions to fill in the partner's form. Step 2: spell the surname and say the email address; the partner writes and checks. Check: Óscar was born on 22 March 2011 in Bilbao; Lucía's email is lucia.garcia@correo.es.
10 minEmail Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Match the Spanish to the English. Watch the formal ones (Le escribo para…, ¿Podría enviarme…?). Answer chain below.
10 minWorksheet (p.6) A and B in pairs. When feeding back on the error correction, students say why (agreement? tú or usted? verb ending?).
5 minExit ticket. Write the first two lines and the last line of an email to a hotel in Spain asking about a room. Homework: play Fix the Email at leim.ie/fix-the-email.html and copy five lines from the "Go back over these" list.
Errors to listen for: Querido Ana (Querida) · a comma after the greeting (Spanish uses a colon) · Te escribo / tu ayuda to a hotel (Le, su) · mixing tú and usted in one email · Gracias para (por) · un foto (una) · Mayo, Irlandés with capitals (mayo, irlandés) · voy a visito (visitar) · missing ¿ and ¡.
Language awareness: Irish letters change register too: A Mháire, a chara to start, Le grá to a friend but Is mise le meas in a formal letter. Ask: which Spanish ending matches Is mise le meas? Support: give weaker students the poster as a mat. Fast finishers: write a formal email to the summer camp asking two questions about it.

Domino answer chain:

  1. Say hello to your family from me. = Saluda a tu familia de mi parte.
  2. Send me a photo. = Mándame una foto.
  3. surnames = los apellidos
  4. date of birth = la fecha de nacimiento
  5. Write to me soon. = Escríbeme pronto.
  6. I'm writing to tell you… = Te escribo para contarte…
  7. Dear Sir, = Estimado señor:
  8. Dear Ana, = Querida Ana:
  9. Thanks for your email. = Gracias por tu correo.
  10. I'm writing to you to… (formal) = Le escribo para…
  11. Love (a hug), = Un abrazo,
  12. Could you send me…? (formal) = ¿Podría enviarme…?
  13. See you soon, = Hasta pronto,
  14. the attachment = el archivo adjunto
  15. Sorry for not writing sooner. = Perdona por no escribir antes.
  16. You ask about… = Me preguntas por…
  17. the subject (of an email) = el asunto
  18. Yours faithfully, = Atentamente,
  19. the @ sign = la arroba
  20. the address = la dirección

Querida Ana:   a colon, not a comma!

The 5-point check
1 Agreement: la ciudad bonita, una foto, tus noticias
2 Verb endings: tengo, fui, jugamos
3 tú or usted? Don't mix te / le, tu / su
4 Infinitive after para, ir a, quiero: voy a visitar
5 ¿ ¡ and accents: ¿Qué? ¿Cuándo? ¡Qué guay!

Worksheet

Iker's email from Pamplona

↗ Léim

A. Read Iker's email

Pamplona, 20 de febrero de 2027
Querido Seán: ¡Hola! Me llamo Iker y voy a ser tu compañero en el intercambio de abril. Tengo quince años y vivo en Pamplona con mis padres y mi hermana pequeña, Ane, que tiene ocho años. Hablo español y euskera, y estudio inglés en el instituto, pero hablo fatal. Me encanta el baloncesto: entreno tres veces a la semana. El verano pasado vi el encierro de San Fermín desde el balcón de mi tío. ¡Fue increíble! Cuando vengas en abril, vamos a ir de excursión a los Pirineos con mi familia, y mi madre va a hacer tortilla de patatas para ti. Tengo unas preguntas: ¿Qué comida no te gusta? ¿Tienes alergias? ¿Qué deportes practicas? Te mando una foto de mi familia en el archivo adjunto. Mi madre dice que tienes que rellenar la ficha del intercambio antes del 15 de marzo. Escríbeme pronto. Un abrazo, Iker
  1. When is the exchange?
  2. Who does Iker live with?
  3. Which languages does he speak?
  4. What does he say about his English?
  5. How often does he train, and what sport?
  6. Where did he watch the encierro from?
  7. What are they going to do in April? Give two things.
  8. What three questions does Iker ask?
  9. What is attached to the email?
  10. What does Seán have to do, and by when?

B. Find and fix the mistake

  1. Querido Sara:
  2. Gracias para tu correo.
  3. Mi hermana es muy simpático.
  4. Ayer jugé al baloncesto.
  1. Te mando un foto de mi casa.
  2. En verano voy a visito Madrid.
  3. Estimado señor: ¿Tienes piscina?
  4. Mi amigo es Irlandés.

C. Now you

Write Seán's reply to Iker (60–80 words). Start and end it correctly, answer Iker's three questions, and say what you did last weekend. Then do the 5-point check from the poster.

Answers. Do you have any allergies? What sports do you do? 9 A photo of his family. 10 Fill in the exchange form before 15 March. · B: 11 Querida 12 por 13 simpática 14 jugué 15 una 16 visitar 17 ¿Tiene…? (formal: usted) 18 irlandés (no capital).
Watch out: el intercambio = the exchange; hablo fatal = I speak really badly; el encierro = the running of the bulls; cuando vengas = when you come; rellenar la ficha = fill in the form.
