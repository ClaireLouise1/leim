Opposite sides equal, opposite angles equal (Theorem 9)
The diagonals bisect each other (Theorem 10)
The angle in a semicircle is 90° (Corollary 3)

1. Words of proof

Axiom: accepted without proof. Theorem: proved. Corollary: follows quickly from a theorem. Converse: swap "if" and "then" (not always true!). Implies (⇒): if … then …. Proof: steps, each with a reason.

2. Parallelograms

Opposite sides equal, opposite angles equal, neighbouring angles add to 180° (co-interior), and the diagonals bisect each other. Converses: opposite angles equal, OR opposite sides equal, OR diagonals bisecting each other → it IS a parallelogram.

3. Circles

[BC] a diameter ⇒ ∠BAC = 90° (Corollary 3), and the converse (Corollary 4). Higher Level: angle at the centre = 2 × angle at the circle on the same arc (Theorem 19); angles on the same arc are equal (Corollary 2); opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add to 180° (Corollary 5).

4. Congruent triangles (same shape AND size)

SSS three sides · SAS two sides and the angle between them · ASA two angles and the side between them · RHS right angle, hypotenuse, one other side. Not tests: AAA, and SSA (angle not between the sides). △ABC ≡ △PQR: A↔P, B↔Q, C↔R.

5. Similar triangles (same shape)

Equal angles ⇒ similar ⇒ sides in proportion (Theorem 13). Scale factor = new side ÷ matching old side. DE ∥ BC makes △ADE similar to △ABC. Higher Level: DE ∥ BC cuts [AB] and [AC] in the same ratio (Theorem 12).

Classic mistakes: adding instead of multiplying for similar triangles · using |AD| instead of the whole |AB| · SAS with the angle not between the sides · matching the letters in the wrong order · 180° for the angle in a semicircle.
Exam tip: one fact per line with a reason in brackets. In a congruence proof list three facts, then name the test: "△ABD ≡ △ACD (SSS)".

C. Congruent triangles

  1. Which test proves △ABC ≡ △PQR, if any? |AB| = |PQ| = 5 cm, |BC| = |QR| = 7 cm, |AC| = |PR| = 9 cm.
  2. Which test proves △ABC ≡ △PQR, if any? |AB| = |PQ| = 6 cm, ∠ABC = ∠PQR = 50°, |BC| = |QR| = 8 cm.
  3. Which test proves △ABC ≡ △PQR, if any? ∠A = ∠P = 40°, ∠B = ∠Q = 75°, |AB| = |PQ| = 10 cm.
  4. Which test proves △ABC ≡ △PQR, if any? ∠A = ∠P = 90°, |BC| = |QR| = 13 cm, |AB| = |PQ| = 5 cm.
  5. Which test proves △ABC ≡ △PQR, if any? ∠A = ∠P = 50°, ∠B = ∠Q = 60°, ∠C = ∠R = 70°.
  6. △ABC ≡ △XYZ. Which side is equal to [BC]?
  7. △ABC ≡ △XYZ and ∠ABC = 64°. Find ∠XYZ.
  8. What is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle?

D. Similar triangles

  1. Use the diagram (DE ∥ BC). Find x = |BC|.
  2. In the diagram, |AE| = 3 cm. Find |AC|.
  3. A triangle has sides 4 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm. In a similar triangle the shortest side is 10 cm. Find the longest side.
  4. Are triangles with sides 3, 5, 6 and 6, 10, 12 similar?
  5. One triangle has angles 50° and 60°; another has angles 60° and 70°. Are they similar?
  6. Seán is 1.8 m tall and his shadow is 2.4 m long. At the same time a building's shadow is 16 m long. How tall is the building?
  7. Higher Level: In △ABC, DE ∥ BC and |AD| : |DB| = 3 : 2. |AC| = 15 cm. Find |AE|.

E. Exam-style questions (show your work, with reasons)

  1. A parallelogram. ABCD is a parallelogram with ∠ABC = 72°. Its diagonals meet at M. |AM| = 3.5 cm and |BD| = 11 cm. Find (a) ∠BCD (b) ∠ADC (c) |AC| (d) |BM|. Give a reason for each.
  2. A proof. [AB] and [CD] bisect each other at E (see the diagram). (a) Prove that △AEC ≡ △BED. (b) Hence show that AC ∥ DB.
  3. Similar triangles. In △PQR, S is on [PQ], T is on [PR] and ST ∥ QR. |PS| = 8 cm, |SQ| = 4 cm and |ST| = 10 cm. (a) Find |QR|. (b) |PR| = 18 cm. Find |PT|.
