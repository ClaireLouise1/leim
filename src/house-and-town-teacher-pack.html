Where I live: house & town

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 1.5, 1.10, 1.19, 3.1 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 1.12, 1.16, 3.1 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can describe their home and town (vivo en…, hay…, no hay…), say where things are with prepositions (encima de, debajo de, al lado de, entre…), choose hay or está, use del and al, and understand common signs and flat adverts.

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Spot the Difference bedrooms (two cards) · p.3–4 House & Town Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 1st–3rd year. Time: about 60 minutes.

Procedure

5 minLead-in: hide and seek. Before class, hide a pencil case. Students guess where it is: ¿Está debajo de la mesa? ¿Está en la mochila de Seán? Answer only sí / no / caliente / frío. Board the prepositions as they come up.
10 minGuided discovery. Board: En mi dormitorio hay una cama. / La cama está al lado de la ventana. / Hay dos supermercados. / Las llaves están en la mesa. Ask: "What comes straight after hay? What comes before está?" (hay + un / una / numbers; el / la / mi + está). Then encima del sofá · voy al cine: "What happened to de + el and a + el?" Concept checks: "Hay el parque: right or wrong?" "Mi casa es cerca del colegio, or está?" (location → estar).
5 minPoster drill (p.5). Move a real object around your desk; the class says where it is in chorus. Then pairs: one hides a rubber, the other asks ¿Dónde está la goma?
15 minSpot the Difference (p.2), pairs A and B, back to back. Each describes their bedroom picture and asks questions until they find the 6 differences. They write each difference as a sentence: En mi dibujo el gato está encima de la cama, pero en tu dibujo está debajo.
10 minHouse & Town Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Deal the dominoes; players match a Spanish sentence to its English. First to place all their dominoes wins. Answer chain below.
10 minWorksheet (p.6) A and B in pairs. Feed back on the error correction.
5 minExit ticket. Three sentences about your home or town: one with hay, one with está + a preposition, one opinion with porque. Homework: play Room Raider at leim.ie/room-raider.html and screenshot your best score.
Errors to listen for: Hay el parque (un parque) · En mi casa están tres dormitorios (hay) · Mi casa es cerca de… (está) · Las llaves está (están) · de el / a el (del / al) · Vivo en un casa (una) · Comparto de mi hermano (con) · la librería for the library (la biblioteca; una librería is a bookshop).
Spot the Difference answers: 1 A: the cat is on the bed · B: under the bed. 2 A: the ball is under the desk · B: under the chair. 3 A: the books are on the desk · B: on the bed. 4 A: the guitar is next to the bed · B: on the wardrobe. 5 A: the phone is on the chair · B: on the desk. 6 A: one window · B: two windows.
Culture note: most Spaniards in towns and cities live in a piso in a block, often with a balcony and a lift (ascensor); houses with gardens are more common on the outskirts. Many towns have a plaza mayor, and families go out for el paseo in the evening. Support: weaker students use the poster as a mat. Fast finishers: draw and describe their dream bedroom.

Domino answer chain:

  1. There isn't much to do. = No hay mucho que hacer.
  2. The kitchen is on the ground floor. = La cocina está en la planta baja.
  3. I share my bedroom with my brother. = Comparto mi dormitorio con mi hermano.
  4. The keys are between the books and the lamp. = Las llaves están entre los libros y la lámpara.
  5. I go to the swimming pool. = Voy a la piscina.
  6. The chemist's is opposite the bank. = La farmacia está enfrente del banco.
  7. My area is very quiet. = Mi barrio es muy tranquilo.
  8. My bedroom is upstairs. = Mi dormitorio está arriba.
  9. I live on the outskirts. = Vivo en las afueras.
  10. The library is next to the park. = La biblioteca está al lado del parque.
  11. My phone is on the sofa. = Mi móvil está encima del sofá.
  12. In my town there is a castle. = En mi pueblo hay un castillo.
  13. I go to the cinema. = Voy al cine.
  14. The cat is under the bed. = El gato está debajo de la cama.
  15. There are three bedrooms. = Hay tres dormitorios.
  16. The dog is behind the sofa. = El perro está detrás del sofá.
  17. I live in the countryside. = Vivo en el campo.
  18. I live in an apartment. = Vivo en un piso.
  19. Apartment for rent. = Se alquila piso.
  20. It's ten minutes away on foot. = Está a diez minutos a pie.

Worksheet

Íñigo's flat in San Sebastián

↗ Léim

A. Read Íñigo's email

¡Hola! ¿Qué tal? Te escribo desde San Sebastián, en el norte de España. Vivo con mi madre y mi abuelo en un piso antiguo en la Parte Vieja, el barrio más antiguo de la ciudad. El piso está en el cuarto piso y no hay ascensor, ¡así que subo muchas escaleras todos los días! Hay dos dormitorios, un salón pequeño, una cocina y un cuarto de baño. Duermo en el salón, en un sofá cama, pero no me importa porque desde la ventana veo el mar. En mi barrio hay muchos bares de pintxos, una iglesia muy bonita y un mercado, y la playa de la Concha está a cinco minutos a pie. Lo malo es que en verano hay muchos turistas y es muy ruidoso. El año que viene vamos a vivir en las afueras, en una casa con jardín, y voy a tener mi propio dormitorio. ¿Y tú? ¿Cómo es tu casa? ¿Qué hay en tu pueblo? Un abrazo, Íñigo
  1. Where is San Sebastián, and who does Íñigo live with?
  2. What is the Parte Vieja?
  3. Which floor is the flat on, and what is the problem?
  4. Which rooms are there?
  5. Where does Íñigo sleep, and why doesn't he mind?
  6. Name three things there are in his area.
  7. How far is the beach, and what is bad about summer?
  8. What will change next year?

B. Find and fix the mistake

  1. Hay el cine en mi pueblo.
  2. El gato está debajo de el sofá.
  3. Los domingos voy a el parque.
  4. Mi casa es cerca de la playa.
  1. Las llaves está en la mesa.
  2. En mi casa están tres dormitorios.
  3. Mi dormitorio es muy luminosa.
  4. Vivo en un casa grande.

C. Now you

Write a reply to Íñigo (40–60 words). Answer his questions: what your home is like and where it is, what rooms there are, where your bedroom is, and what there is (and isn't) in your town. Give an opinion with a reason.

Answers. A: 1 In the north of Spain; his mum and his grandad. 2 The oldest area of the city (the old town). 3 The fourth floor; there's no lift, so he climbs lots of stairs every day. 4 Two bedrooms, a small living room, a kitchen and a bathroom. 5 In the living room, on a sofa bed; he can see the sea from the window. 6 Any three: lots of pintxos bars, a very pretty church, a market (the beach). 7 Five minutes on foot; lots of tourists and it's very noisy. 8 They're moving to a house with a garden on the outskirts, and he'll have his own bedroom. · B: 9 un cine 10 del 11 al 12 está 13 están 14 hay 15 luminoso 16 una.
Watch out: la Parte Vieja = the old town; el ascensor = the lift; subo las escaleras = I climb the stairs; no me importa = I don't mind; los pintxos = Basque tapas; lo malo = the bad thing.
