Junior Cycle Spanish · Teacher pack

Describing people

↗ Léim

Learning outcomes: 1.10, 1.19, 2.1 (2017 spec, exams 2027–2028) · 1.12, 2.1 (2025 spec, 1st years from Sept 2026)

Aims

By the end of the lesson students can describe someone's looks and personality, make adjectives agree (gender and number), choose ser or tener for looks, and ask and answer ¿Cómo es…?

In this pack

p.1 Teacher notes · p.2 Guess Who? speaking game · p.3–4 Adjective Dominoes (cut-up) · p.5 Wall poster · p.6 Worksheet + answers. Level: 1st–2nd year. Time: about 60 minutes.

Procedure

5 minLead-in. Show a picture of a famous person (or quickly sketch one). Students say anything they can: Es alto. Tiene el pelo corto. Write their ideas on the board without correcting yet.
10 minGuided discovery. Board: Mi hermano es alto / Mi hermana es alta / Mis hermanos son altos / Mis hermanas son altas. Ask: "What changes, and why?" Then inteligente for a boy and a girl: "Does it change?" Then trabajador → trabajadora. Concept checks: "Two brothers and one sister: altos or altas?" "Mi madre tiene el pelo largo: why not larga?" (it agrees with el pelo).
5 minPoster drill. Put up the poster (p.5). Say a person (mis primas, mi tío…) and an adjective; students chorus the right form.
15 minGuess Who? (p.2, one copy per student). In pairs, each student secretly chooses a person. Take turns asking yes/no questions: ¿Es alta? ¿Tiene el pelo rizado? ¿Tiene los ojos azules? ¿Lleva gafas? ¿Tiene barba? Answer in full: Sí, es alta. / No, no tiene barba. Cross out people as you go. First to guess the name wins. Play three rounds. Fast pairs: describe a card in three sentences; partner points to it.
10 minAdjective Dominoes (p.3–4), groups of 3–4. Deal out the dominoes; players match a Spanish sentence to its English. First to place all their dominoes wins. Answer chain below.
10 minWorksheet (p.6) A and B in pairs. Feed back on the error correction.
5 minExit ticket. Describe a best friend or a famous person in three sentences: looks, personality, and one pero…. Homework: play Agreement Snap at leim.ie/agreement-snap.html and screenshot your best score.
Errors to listen for: Mi hermana es simpático · Mis amigos son divertido · Es el pelo largo (should be tiene) · tiene el pelo larga · los ojos azul · es trabajador for a girl · a girl writing Soy alto.
Sensitivity: don't ask students to describe classmates' looks; use the Guess Who? characters, famous people or invented people, and leave out words like gordo and feo. Support: give the endings table from the poster as a strip. Fast finishers: add no … nada and a veces.

Worksheet

Describing people

↗ Léim

A. Read Aoife's email

¡Hola! Me llamo Aoife y tengo trece años. Te voy a describir a mi mejor amiga. Se llama Carla y es española. Es alta y tiene el pelo largo, rizado y castaño. Tiene los ojos verdes y lleva gafas. Carla es muy divertida y generosa, pero a veces es un poco habladora en clase. Mis padres dicen que es muy educada. Su hermano, Marcos, es bajo y tiene el pelo corto y rubio. Es deportista pero bastante tímido. ¿Y tú? ¿Cómo es tu mejor amigo o amiga? Un abrazo, Aoife
  1. How old is Aoife?
  2. Describe Carla's hair (three details).
  3. What colour are Carla's eyes, and what does she wear?
  4. What is the one negative thing about Carla?
  5. What do Aoife's parents say about Carla?
  6. Describe Marcos: looks and personality.

B. Find and fix the mistake

  1. Carla es muy divertido.
  2. Marcos es el pelo corto.
  3. Mis amigas son simpáticos.
  4. Tiene los ojos verde.
  1. Mi madre es trabajador.
  2. Mis hermanos es altos.
  3. Mi abuela tiene el pelo blanca.
  4. Mi profesor son muy serio.

C. Now you

Write a reply to Aoife (40–60 words). Describe your best friend (or a famous person): looks, personality and one pero….

Answers. A: 1 13. 2 Long, curly, brown. 3 Green; she wears glasses. 4 Sometimes she's a bit chatty in class. 5 She's very polite. 6 Short, with short blond hair; sporty but quite shy. · B: 7 divertida 8 tiene 9 simpáticas 10 verdes 11 trabajadora 12 son 13 blanco 14 es.
Watch out: educada = polite (not "educated").
