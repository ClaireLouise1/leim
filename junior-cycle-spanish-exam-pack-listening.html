Exam Pack listening

Press play and your device reads each listening item aloud in Spanish, twice, with a pause in between, like the real exam. Use it with Part 3 (pages 15–16) and the mock paper Section A (pages 17–18) of the Léim Exam Pack.

Practice sets 1–4Mock paper A1–A6No sound?
No Spanish voice found on this device. Try Chrome, Safari or Edge, or a phone or tablet. On a computer you may need to add a Spanish voice (see No sound?). The scripts are also at the back of the Exam Pack for an adult to read aloud.

How to use it: read the questions in the pack first (30 seconds), press ▶ Play twice, and write your answers. Keep the script closed until you have checked your answers with the answer key. The voice is your device's built-in Spanish voice, so it can sound a little robotic, and it may differ from phone to phone.

Part 3: Practice sets 1–4

Mock paper: Section A (Listening, 140 marks)

Do these in one go, with the timer, after the student has had 30 seconds to read each question. No scripts until the end.

No sound?

  • Turn the volume up and check the phone isn't on silent (on an iPhone, flick the switch on the side).
  • Use Chrome, Safari or Edge. Some other browsers have no Spanish voice.
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Spanish, and download a Spain voice (e.g. Mónica).
  • Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices → Spanish (Spain).
  • Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices → Spanish (Spain).
  • Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech → Google speech engine → Install voice data → Spanish (Spain).
  • Still nothing? The scripts are at the back of the Exam Pack (pages 28–31): a parent or friend can read them aloud.
