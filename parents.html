Help your teen revise

Everything on Léim is free to use. Your teen just plays: no account and no email address needed.

How it works

  • Game first. Each topic starts with a short game. Your teen tries it, then checks the rules underneath.
  • Mistakes become revision. The end of each game lists what they got wrong, so they know what to go back over.
  • A free revision sheet for every topic, to print.
