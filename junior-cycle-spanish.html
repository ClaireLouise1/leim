What's in every unit

Seven parts, always in the same order, each tagged to the learning outcomes it covers.

Short lesson

A 5–8 minute lesson on the unit's core language.

Vocabulary set

Flashcards with audio to build the word bank for reading and listening.

Listening practice

Audio clips with exam-style questions.

Reading practice

Short texts with questions that mirror the exam.

Writing task

A model email, note, postcard or blog post, with a checklist of what scores marks.

Grammar drill

A self-marking quiz on the unit's grammar point.

Speaking prompts

Question banks and sample answers to prepare for the oral CBA.
