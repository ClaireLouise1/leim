About Léim

Léim is the Irish word for a leap. That's what good revision should feel like: a clear jump forward, not hours of rereading notes.

Léim is an online revision school for Irish secondary students. It's built by a language teacher with over 25 years' experience teaching at university level, using the same approach that works in the classroom: short explanations, lots of practice, and plenty of chances to use the language.

What makes it different

  • Mapped to the curriculum. Every lesson and quiz is tied to a learning outcome in the official specification.
  • Exam-shaped practice. Tasks look like the real exam, so there are no surprises in June.
  • Short sessions. Fifteen minutes is enough to make progress.

What's coming

Junior Cycle Spanish and Maths are complete, and Junior Cycle English is being added now. Irish comes next, then Leaving Cert revision.

A work in progress

Léim is new and growing every week, and every game, sheet and pack is written from scratch. We check everything carefully, but if you spot a mistake or something that doesn't match what your class is doing, please tell us at hello@leim.ie and we'll fix it quickly.

Questions

Is Léim open now?

Yes. Junior Cycle English, Maths and Spanish are live, with free games for every topic, topic checklists and Exam Centres. Irish is next, then Leaving Cert. Sign up to hear about new subjects and revision tips.

Which specification do you follow?

Junior Cycle Spanish content follows the official specification. Students who started first year in September 2026 are on the revised 2025 specification; older year groups are on the 2017 one. The exam topics overlap heavily, so the revision content works for both, and we flag anything that differs. Junior Cycle Maths follows the 2018 Mathematics specification, which all current students are on.

Is it for students or parents?

Students use it; parents (and teachers) sign up. Students never need to create an account or give an email address.

Are you connected to the SEC or the NCCA?

No. Léim is an independent revision service. We use the published specifications and past exam formats so practice matches what students will see.
