Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026. Plain-English summary: we only collect what we need to send you the free guide and news about Léim, we never sell it, and you can unsubscribe any time.

Who we are

Léim (leim.ie) is an online revision service based in Cork, Ireland. You can contact us at hello@leim.ie.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect your first name, your email address, and whether you're a parent/guardian or a teacher.

Why we use it

  • To send you the free guide you asked for.
  • To send you updates about Léim, including when it opens and any launch offers.

We do this on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email or by emailing us.

Children

Only adults (parents, guardians and teachers) can join the waitlist. We don't ask students to create accounts or give us their email address, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Who we share it with

We use MailerLite to store the waitlist and send emails. MailerLite processes the data on our behalf and is not allowed to use it for anything else. We never sell or rent your data.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Email hello@leim.ie. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.
