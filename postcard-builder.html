Holidays & travel

Spot the pattern: one holiday, three times

  • Normalmente voy a Kerry con mi familia. (Usually I go to Kerry with my family.)
  • El verano pasado fui a Francia. (Last summer I went to France.)
  • El verano que viene voy a ir a Italia. (Next summer I'm going to go to Italy.)

Look at the words at the start of each sentence, then at the verb. What tells you when? Think about it, then check.

Show me

The time phrase and the verb go together. Habits use the present (voy, viajo, me alojo). Finished trips use the preterite (fui, viajé, me alojé). Plans use ir a + infinitive (voy a ir, voy a viajar). In the exam, spot the time phrase first: it tells you which tense is coming.

Getting there and where to stay

en avión (plane) · en tren · en barco (boat / ferry) · en coche · en autocar (coach) · en bici · but a pie (on foot). El viaje duró tres horas (the journey lasted three hours). Fue largo / cómodo / aburrido.

Me alojo en… / Me alojé en… un hotel · un camping (campsite) · un albergue juvenil (youth hostel) · un apartamento · una casa rural (country house) · la casa de mis tíos (my aunt and uncle's house). El hotel está cerca de la playa. Hay una piscina.

Writing a postcard

Start: Querido Pablo: / Querida Ana: / Queridos abuelos: (it agrees!) or ¡Hola desde Málaga! End: Un abrazo (a hug) · Besos (kisses) · Hasta pronto (see you soon).

The postcard checklist: where and who with · the journey · where you're staying · the weather · what you did (past) · what you're going to do (future) · an opinion (lo estoy pasando bomba = I'm having a great time).

Culture: in Spain, most people take their holidays in August: many city shops and offices close, and families head to la costa or back to el pueblo, the village where their family comes from. A puente ("bridge") is a long weekend made by taking a day off between a public holiday and the weekend. Lots of people also walk part of the Camino de Santiago. Compare: where do Irish families usually go in summer?
Exam tip: In Writing, a postcard or message about a holiday is a favourite task. Tick off the checklist and show off three tenses: ¡Hola desde Málaga! Estoy de vacaciones con mi familia. Viajamos en avión y me alojo en un hotel cerca de la playa. Hace sol y mucho calor. Ayer fui a la playa y probé la paella. Mañana voy a visitar el castillo. ¡Lo estoy pasando bomba! Un abrazo, Ciara. In Listening and Reading, catch the details: which country, how they travelled, the weather and whether it's past, present or future.
