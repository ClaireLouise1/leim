Exam skills: Listening & Reading

Spot the pattern: two questions

📢 Aviso
Instituto San Jorge: excursión a Madrid el jueves doce de marzo. El autobús sale del instituto a las ocho en punto. Precio: veinticinco euros, comida incluida.

Question 1: What is this notice about?   Question 2: At what time does the bus leave?
To answer one of them you need the whole text. For the other you need just three words. Which is which? What did you look for each time?

Show me

Question 1 is a gist question: the big picture (a school trip). You get it from the title, the type of text and a few key words (excursión, autobús, precio). You don't need every word. Question 2 is a detail question: one fact (8 o'clock). You scan for the time (a las…) and ignore the rest. Knowing which kind of question you have tells you how to read or listen.

Gist or detail?

🔭 Gist🔍 Detail
It asksWhat is it about? What kind of text? Where would you hear it? How does the writer feel overall?When? Where? How much? How many? Who? Why? Name two things…
You…skim: the title, the first and last lines, words you know, the tonescan: look or listen for one kind of word (a time, a price, a day) and stop there
Cluesthe text type (📱 text, 📢 notice, 📻 announcement), pictures, greetings like Querido… or Atención, señores pasajerosquestion words: ¿Cuándo? ¿Dónde? ¿Quién? ¿Por qué? ¿Cuánto? ¿A qué hora?

Before, during and after

  1. Before: read the questions first and underline the question word. Predict the kind of answer: a time? a price? a place? a reason? Look at the title and any pictures.
  2. During: first time through, get the gist. Next time, go for the details. If you hear a piece more than once, use each listen for a different job. Don't panic at words you don't know: keep going.
  3. After: check every answer. Does it answer the question word? (A "when?" question needs a time or day.) Did you give the number of points asked ("name two things")? Is it sensible? (€550 for a T-shirt? Look again.) Never leave a blank: a smart guess can still get marks.

Traps examiners love

  • Numbers that sound alike: cuatro 4 · catorce 14 · cuarenta 40 · seis 6 · dieciséis 16 · sesenta 60 · setenta 70 · quince 15 · cincuenta 50 · doce 12 · dos 2.
  • Times: menos cuarto = quarter to (las nueve menos cuarto = 8:45, not 9:45). y media = half past.
  • Prices: doce euros con cincuenta = €12.50.
  • Days and months: martes (Tue) / miércoles (Wed), junio (June) / julio (July).
  • Change of mind: the first thing you hear is often not the answer. Listen for pero, sin embargo, al final, ahora, no… sino, perdón: El partido no es el sábado; es el domingo. → Sunday.
Irish and English link: it's the same skill as your Irish cluastuiscint and léamhthuiscint, and English comprehension. Read the questions first, find the key word, check your answer. You already know how to do this!
Exam tip: Listening (140 marks) and Reading (140 marks) are 280 of the 360 marks on the JC Spanish paper, so these skills are worth more than any one topic. Answer in English when the question is in English, don't copy chunks of Spanish, and give exactly what's asked: one detail, two details, a time or a reason.
