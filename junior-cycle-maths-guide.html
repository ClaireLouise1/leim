How the exam works

One written paper in June, at Higher or Ordinary level, set and marked by the State Examinations Commission.

2 hoursfor the Higher and the Ordinary paper (2026)
270marks at both levels (2026)
13questions, and every one must be answered: there is no choice
1+ CBAat least one in class (a Mathematical or a Statistical Investigation; most schools do one), reported separately on the JCPA

Because every question is compulsory, every topic matters. See exactly how the Higher and Ordinary papers are marked, question by question, in the Maths Exam Centre.

Questions

Which specification do you follow?

The Junior Cycle Mathematics specification (2018), which all current 1st, 2nd and 3rd years follow. Every game, sheet and pack is tagged with its learning outcome codes (for example N.1a), so you can match it to your child's school work.

Higher or Ordinary level?

The games and sheets work for both levels. Where a topic has extra Higher level parts, we say so on the topic page.

Do students need an account?

No. Students just play. Parents and teachers can sign up for the free parent guide and news of new topics.

Are you connected to the SEC or the NCCA?

No. Léim is an independent revision service. All questions are written by us in the style of the exam; we never copy past papers.
